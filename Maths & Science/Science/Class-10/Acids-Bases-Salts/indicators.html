Bina Chakhe Kaise Pata Chale? 🎨

Lab mein rakhi do saaf botlon mein se ek acid hai aur ek base — par dono dekhne mein bilkul ek jaise (rangheen paani). Chakh toh sakte nahi (khatarnaak hai!). Toh pata kaise karein? Indicators se!

📌 Indicator: Aisa padarth jo acid aur base ke saath alag-alag rang (ya gandh) dikhakar batata hai ki cheez acidic hai ya basic.

🌈 Rang Badalne Wale Indicators

IndicatorAcid meinBase mein
Blue litmusLaal (red)Neela hi rehta hai
Red litmusLaal hi rehta haiNeela (blue)
Methyl orangeLaal / gulaabiPeela (yellow)
PhenolphthaleinRangheen (colourless)Gulaabi (pink)

🧠 Yaad rakhne ki trick: "Acid turns blue litmus Red" — Acid → Red. Aur phenolphthalein base mein gulaabi (pink) — "Pink for baP" (base). Bas ye do yaad ho gaye toh aadha chapter ho gaya!

🌿 Litmus Aata Kahan Se Hai?

Litmus koi factory mein banaya gaya rasayan nahi hai — ye lichen naam ke ek prakritik jeev se nikaala jaata hai (jo chattano aur pedon par ugta hai)! Uska rang paani mein baingani (purple) hota hai, aur acid-base ke hisaab se laal ya neela ho jaata hai. Prakriti ka apna chemistry kit.

👚 Haldi Ka Daag Laal Kyun Ho Jaata Hai?

Kapde par haldi gir gayi — peela daag. Ab aapne us par sabun ragda, aur daag achanak laal-bhoora ho gaya! Ghabraiye mat — sabun ek base hai, aur haldi ek indicator. Base ke saath haldi laal ho jaati hai. Ab us par thoda nimbu ya sirka (acid) daal do — daag wapas peela hokar dhul jaayega! Ye pura chemistry ka jaadu hai.

👃 Olfactory Indicators — Gandh Wale

Kuch indicators rang nahi badalte — unki gandh (smell) badalti hai. Inhe olfactory indicators kehte hain.

CheezAcid meinBase mein
Pyaaz (onion)Apni gandh rehti haiGandh khatm ho jaati hai
Vanilla essenceGandh rehti haiGandh khatm
Laung (clove) ka telGandh rehti haiGandh khatm
👀 Olfactory Indicators Ka Khaas Faayda

Ye un bachchon ke liye bahut kaam ke hain jo dekh nahi sakte (visually impaired) — wo rang toh nahi dekh sakte, par gandh ke badlav se acid-base ka farak aasani se samajh sakte hain. Isiliye NCERT inhe khaas taur par sikhata hai. Science sabke liye ho, tabhi asli science hai.

📋 Universal Indicator

Upar wale indicators sirf itna batate hain ki cheez acid hai ya base. Par kitni acidic hai? Iske liye universal indicator use hota hai — ye kai indicators ka mishran hai jo pH ke hisaab se poori rainbow dikhata hai: laal (bahut acidic) → peela → hara (neutral) → neela → baingani (bahut basic). Iski poori kahani Lesson 5 mein.
