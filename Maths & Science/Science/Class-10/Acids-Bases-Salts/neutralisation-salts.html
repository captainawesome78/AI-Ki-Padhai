Jab Acid Aur Base Mile 🤝

Acid ke paas H⁺ hai, base ke paas OH⁻. Jab dono milte hain, toh H⁺ aur OH⁻ judkar paani (H₂O) ban jaate hain — aur bache hue ions milkar salt (lavan). Dono ka "khatta-pan" aur "kadwa-pan" khatm ho jaata hai — isiliye ise neutralisation kehte hain.

📐 Acid + Base → Salt + Paani
NaOH + HCl → NaCl + H₂O

Ion level par asli kahani:
H⁺ + OH⁻ → H₂O

🔥 Neutralisation Exothermic Hai

Acid aur base milaate waqt bartan garam ho jaata hai — kyunki neutralisation ek exothermic reaction (abhikriya) hai (garmi chhodti hai). Isiliye lab mein inhe dheere-dheere, hilate hue milaya jaata hai.

🧂 Salt Kya Hai?

"Salt" sunte hi khane wala namak yaad aata hai — par chemistry mein salt ka matlab bahut bada hai:

📌 Salt: Acid ke H⁺ ki jagah kisi metal (dhaatu) ion ke aa jaane se bana padarth. Yani acid + base ki reaction ka product (paani ke alawa).

AcidBaseSalt bana
HClNaOHNaCl (sodium chloride)
H₂SO₄NaOHNa₂SO₄ (sodium sulphate)
HNO₃KOHKNO₃ (potassium nitrate)
HClNH₄OHNH₄Cl (ammonium chloride)

👨‍👩‍👧 Salt Ke Parivaar (Families)

Jin salts mein koi ek ion common ho, wo ek hi family ke maane jaate hain:

• Sodium family: NaCl, Na₂SO₄, NaNO₃ (sab mein Na⁺)
• Chloride family: NaCl, KCl, CaCl₂ (sab mein Cl⁻)

⚖️ Har Salt Neutral Nahi Hota!

Ye Class 10 ka sabse tricky par sabse zyada poocha jaane wala point hai. Salt ka pH is baat par depend karta hai ki wo kis taakat ke acid aur base se bana:

Jo parent zyada strong hota hai, salt ka swabhaav usi ki taraf jhukta hai.
Kis se banaSalt kaisapHUdaharan
Strong acid + Strong baseNeutral= 7NaCl
Strong acid + Weak baseAcidic< 7NH₄Cl
Weak acid + Strong baseBasic> 7Na₂CO₃, CH₃COONa

🧠 Trick: Jo zyada strong hai, salt uska hi swabhav (nature) le leta hai! Strong acid jeeta → acidic salt. Strong base jeeta → basic salt. Dono barabar → neutral.

🧂 Khane Wala Namak (NaCl) — Sabse Kaam Ka Salt

Common salt strong acid (HCl) aur strong base (NaOH) se banta hai, isliye neutral (pH = 7) hai. Ye samundar ke paani aur zameen ke andar "rock salt" ke roop mein milta hai.

📌 NaCl sirf khane ke kaam nahi aata — ye kai zaroori rasayanon ka kaccha maal (raw material) hai: sodium hydroxide, bleaching powder, baking soda aur washing soda — sab isi se bante hain! (Poori detail Lesson 6 mein.)

🩺 Khane mein iodine wala namak (iodised salt) istemal karna zaroori hai. Iodine ki kami se ghenga (goitre) bimari aur bachchon mein dimaagi vikas ki samasya ho sakti hai. Isiliye Bharat sarkar ne iodised salt ko badhava diya hai.

🇮🇳 Namak Satyagraha

1930 mein Mahatma Gandhi ne Sabarmati Ashram se Dandi tak 390 km paidal chalkar samundar ke paani se namak banaya — angrezon ke namak kanoon ko todne ke liye. Ek saadharan salt (NaCl) Bharat ki azaadi ki ladai ka prateek ban gaya. Chemistry sirf lab mein nahi, itihaas mein bhi hai!
