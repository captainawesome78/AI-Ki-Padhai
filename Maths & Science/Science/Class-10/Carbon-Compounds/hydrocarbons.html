Sirf Carbon Aur Hydrogen ⛽

Wo compounds jo sirf carbon aur hydrogen se bane hain, unhe hydrocarbons kehte hain. Petrol, diesel, LPG, CNG, mombatti ka mom — sab hydrocarbons hain. Inhe do bade parivaaron mein baanta jaata hai.

📌 Saturated (santript): Carbon ke beech sirf single bond.
📌 Unsaturated (asantript): Kam se kam ek double ya triple bond.

1️⃣ Alkanes — Saturated (Cₙ H₂ₙ₊₂)

Sirf single bonds. Ye kam reactive hote hain — isiliye inhe "paraffins" bhi kehte hain (Latin: parum affinis = kam lagav).

Alkane, alkene aur alkyne — antar sirf carbon ke beech ke bond ka hai.
NaamFormulaStructureKahan milta hai

EthaneC₂H₆C—CPrakritik gas
PropaneC₃H₈C—C—CLPG ka hissa

🧮 Formula check karo: CₙH₂ₙ₊₂ mein n = carbon ki sankhya.
Butane: n = 4 → H = 2(4) + 2 = 10 → C₄H₁₀ ✓

2️⃣ Alkenes — Double Bond (Cₙ H₂ₙ)

NaamFormulaStructure
EtheneC₂H₄CH₂ = CH₂
PropeneC₃H₆CH₃ — CH = CH₂
ButeneC₄H₈CH₃ — CH₂ — CH = CH₂
🍌 Kele Pakane Wali Gas

Ethene (C₂H₄) ek prakritik "ripening hormone" hai — phal khud isse chhodte hain aur isi se pakte hain! Isiliye ek paka kela doosre kacche phalon ke saath rakhne se wo bhi jaldi pak jaate hain. Vyapari isi liye godown mein ethene ka istemal karte hain.

⚠️ Par calcium carbide (masala) se pakana Bharat mein kanoonan mana hai — usme arsenic aur phosphorus ki milaawat hoti hai jo sehat ke liye khatarnaak hai.

3️⃣ Alkynes — Triple Bond (Cₙ H₂ₙ₋₂)

NaamFormulaStructure
Ethyne (acetylene)C₂H₂CH ≡ CH
PropyneC₃H₄CH₃ — C ≡ CH

🔥 Ethyne (acetylene) oxygen ke saath jalkar 3000°C tak ki lau deta hai — isiliye oxy-acetylene welding se loha kaata aur joda jaata hai. Dhyan do: ise hawa mein jalao toh kaala dhuaan deta hai, par shuddh oxygen mein saaf neeli lau — tabhi itni garmi milti hai.

🔀 Isomers — Ek Formula, Alag Structure

📌 Isomers: Aise compounds jinka molecular formula ek jaisa ho, par structure alag ho — aur isi wajah se unke gun bhi alag hote hain.

✔ Butane ke 2 isomers (dono C₄H₁₀)

1. n-Butane (seedhi chain):
CH₃ — CH₂ — CH₂ — CH₃   (B.P. −0.5°C)

2. Isobutane (shakha wali):
CH₃ — CH(CH₃) — CH₃   (B.P. −11.7°C)

Dono mein 4 carbon aur 10 hydrogen — par structure alag, isliye ubalne ka taapmaan bhi alag!

CompoundFormulaKitne isomers
Methane, Ethane, PropaneCH₄, C₂H₆, C₃H₈1 (koi isomer nahi)
ButaneC₄H₁₀2
PentaneC₅H₁₂3
HexaneC₆H₁₄5

💡 Kyun pehle teen ke isomers nahi hote? Kyunki 3 ya usse kam carbon ko shakha (branch) mein sajaane ka koi doosra tareeka hai hi nahi — har tareeka ghumakar dekho toh wahi seedhi chain nikalti hai. Isomerism 4 carbon se shuru hoti hai. Ye board mein poocha jaata hai!

🧪 Saturated Ya Unsaturated — Kaise Pehchaanein?

TestSaturated (alkane)Unsaturated (alkene/alkyne)
Jalne par lauSaaf neeli lauPeeli lau + kaala dhuaan
Bromine water testRang nahi badaltaBromine ka bhoora rang gayab ho jaata hai

🔬 Bromine water test board ka favourite hai. Unsaturated compound double/triple bond ke through bromine ko jod (add) leta hai, isliye bhoora rang khatm ho jaata hai. Saturated compound ke paas jodne ki jagah nahi hai — rang waisa ka waisa.
