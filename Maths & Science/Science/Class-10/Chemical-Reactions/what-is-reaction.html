Roz Aapke Aas-Paas Hoti Hain 🍳

Subah doodh ubalte waqt malai jamna, roti tawe par sikna, cut kiya hua seb kaala pad jaana, lohe ki keel par zang lagna, patakhon ka phatna - ye sab chemical reactions hain. Har baar jab koi nayi cheez banti hai jiske gun (properties) purani se alag hote hain, tab samjho ek chemical reaction ho chuki hai.

🍎 Kata Hua Seb Kaala Kyun Padta Hai?

Aapne seb kaata, plate mein rakha, aur 15 minute baad wo bhoora-kaala ho gaya. Kya hua? Seb ke andar ke rasayan hawa ki oxygen se mil gaye aur ek nayi cheez ban gayi - jiska rang alag hai. Isiliye maa kehti hain ki kate hue seb par nimbu nichod do - nimbu ka Vitamin C oxygen ko pehle khud pakad leta hai aur seb ko bachaa leta hai! Ye ghar ka nuskha asal mein gehri chemistry hai.

📌 Chemical Reaction: Wo prakriya jisme ek ya zyada padarth (reactants) badalkar ek ya zyada naye padarth (products) banate hain, jinke gun bilkul alag hote hain.

🔄 Physical Change vs Chemical Change

Board exam mein ye farak baar-baar poocha jaata hai. Sabse aasaan pehchaan: kya nayi cheez bani? aur kya wapas laut sakte hain?

Physical ChangeChemical Change
Koi nayi cheez nahi bantiNayi cheez banti hai (naye gun ke saath)
Aksar wapas ho sakta hai (reversible)Aksar wapas nahi ho sakta (irreversible)
Sirf roop/halat badalti haiPadarth ki banawat (composition) badalti hai
Barf pighalna, paani ubalna, kaanch tootna, cheeni ghulnaDoodh se dahi, loha zang, khana pakna, kagaz jalna
🤔 Ek Tricky Sawaal

Mombatti (candle) jalna - physical hai ya chemical? Dono! Mom ka pighalna physical change hai (thos se drav, wapas jam sakta hai), par mom ka jalna chemical change hai (CO₂ aur paani ban gaye, wapas mom nahi ban sakta). Board mein aisa sawaal aata hai - dhyan se jawaab dena!

👀 Reaction Hui Hai - Kaise Pehchaanein? (5 Nishaaniyan)

Reaction ho rahi hai ya nahi, ye in 5 mein se koi bhi badlav dekhkar pata chal jaata hai:

1️⃣ Rang Badalna (Change in Colour)

Loha safed-chamakdaar tha, zang lagne par bhoora-laal ho gaya. Copper sulphate ka neela ghol, lohe ki keel daalne par hara ho jaata hai.

2️⃣ Gas Nikalna (Evolution of Gas)

Baking soda mein nimbu/sirka daalo - fizzz! Bubbles nikalte hain (CO₂ gas). Zinc par acid daalo toh hydrogen gas nikalti hai.

3️⃣ Temperature Badalna

Bujha hua chuna (quicklime) paani mein daalo - bartan garam ho jaata hai. Kuch reactions mein ulta thandak hoti hai.

4️⃣ Avakshep Banna (Formation of Precipitate)

Do saaf ghol milaao aur achanak ek thos, na-ghulne wala padarth neeche baith jaaye - use precipitate kehte hain. Jaise lead nitrate + potassium iodide se peela precipitate.

5️⃣ Gandh (Smell) Badalna

Doodh kharab hone par khatti gandh, ya khana sadne par badbu - naye padarth bane hain.

In paanch mein se koi ek bhi dikhe, toh chemical reaction ho rahi hai.

🧪 Reactants Aur Products

📌 Reactants: Jo padarth reaction se pehle the (shuruaati cheezein) - teer ke baayein.
📌 Products: Jo naye padarth bane - teer ke daayein.
Teer (→) ka matlab hai "se banta hai", aur uski disha reaction ki disha batati hai.

✔ Example: Magnesium jalna

Magnesium ribbon hawa mein jalti hai aur safed raakh banti hai:
Magnesium + Oxygen → Magnesium oxide
Yahan Magnesium aur Oxygen = reactants, Magnesium oxide = product.

⚠️ Lab safety: Magnesium ribbon jalte waqt bahut tez safed roshni deta hai - usse seedha nahi dekhna chahiye, aankhon ko nuksaan ho sakta hai. Hamesha teacher ki nigrani mein aur thodi door se dekhein.

🇮🇳 India Connection

🇮🇳 Delhi Ka Loha Stambh

Delhi ke Qutub Minar parisar mein khada lauh stambh (Iron Pillar) lagbhag 1600 saal purana hai - aur aaj tak usme zang nahi laga! Duniya bhar ke vaigyanik hairaan hain. Wajah: praachin Bharatiya lohaaron ne isme phosphorus ki khaas maatra rakhi, jisse lohe ki satah par ek patli suraksha parat ban gayi jo zang (chemical reaction) ko rok deti hai. Ye Bharat ki dhaatu-vidya (metallurgy) ka jeeta-jaagta saboot hai.
