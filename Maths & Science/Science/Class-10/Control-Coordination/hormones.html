Khoon Se Chalne Wale Sandesh 🧪

Nerves tez hain, par unka asar bahut kam samay ka hai. Kuch kaam dheere aur lambe samay tak chahiye — jaise bachche ka badhna, ya javaani (puberty) ke badlav. Ye kaam karte hain hormones.

📌 Hormone: Endocrine glands se nikla rasayanik sandeshvahak, jo khoon ke through poore shareer mein failta hai — par asar sirf apne khaas target organ par karta hai.

🔬 Endocrine vs Exocrine glands:
Endocrine — nalikaviheen (ductless), seedhe khoon mein chhodti hain. Jaise thyroid, pituitary.
Exocrine — nali (duct) ke through chhodti hain. Jaise laar granthi, paseena granthi.

💡 Kuch glands dono kaam karti hain — jaise pancreas: insulin khoon mein (endocrine) aur pachan ras nali se (exocrine)!

😱 Adrenaline — Khatre Ka Hormone

🐍 Achanak Saanp Dikhne Par

Raaste mein achanak saanp dikha! Ek pal mein aapka dil tez dhadakne laga, saans tez ho gayi, haath-pair mein taakat aa gayi, aur poora dhyan saanp par lag gaya. Ye sab adrenaline ka kaam hai — wo turant khoon mein aakar shareer ko "lado ya bhaago" (fight or flight) ke liye taiyaar kar deta hai. Khoon muscles ki taraf mod diya jaata hai (isiliye chehra peela pad jaata hai) aur pachan ruk jaata hai — kyunki us waqt khaana pachaane se zyada zaroori hai bachna!

Endocrine glands seedhe KHOON mein hormone chhodti hain — isiliye asar poore shareer par hota hai.

🧂 Iodine wala namak kyun zaroori hai? Thyroxine banane ke liye iodine chahiye. Kami se ghenga hota hai, aur bachchon mein dimaagi vikas bhi ruk sakta hai. Bharat ke pahaadi ilaakon mein mitti mein iodine kam hota hai — isiliye sarkar ne iodised salt ko badhava diya, aur isse laakhon bachche bache hain.

⚖️ Feedback mechanism: Hormone ki maatra apne aap control hoti hai! Jaise khoon mein shakkar badhi → pancreas zyada insulin banata hai → shakkar kam hui → insulin banna kam ho gaya. Ye "thermostat" jaisa self-control hai — isi ko feedback mechanism kehte hain.

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat ko kabhi "duniya ki diabetes rajdhani" kaha jaata tha. Badalti khaan-paan ki aadatein, kam vyayam aur tanaav iske bade kaaran hain. Achhi khabar ye hai ki type-2 diabetes ka khatra roz 30 minute chalne, cheeni kam karne aur saabut anaaj khane se kaafi kam ho jaata hai. Ye chapter sirf exam nahi — apni sehat ke liye bhi hai.
