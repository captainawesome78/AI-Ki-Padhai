Shareer Ki Taar Ki Jaali ⚡

Nervous system ki basic ikai hai neuron — shareer ki sabse lambi cell. Ek neuron kamar se pair ke angoothe tak, lagbhag 1 metre lamba ho sakta hai!

Neuron ke hisse: dendrite (IN) → cell body → axon → terminal (OUT). Sandesh hamesha ek hi disha mein.

📌 Neuron ke hisse:
🌿 Dendrites — sandesh lete hain (information IN)
⚪ Cell body (soma) — sandesh yahan ikattha hota hai
🔌 Axon — sandesh door le jaata hai (information OUT)
🔚 Axon terminals — agle cell ko sandesh dete hain

📨 Sandesh Ka Safar

1️⃣ Dendrite par: uddipan se ek rasayanik badlav hota hai, jo bijli ka signal (impulse) ban jaata hai.

2️⃣ Axon par: ye bijli ka signal tezi se aage daudta hai.

3️⃣ Axon ke sire par: signal ek rasayan (chemical) chhodta hai.

4️⃣ Synapse paar karke: wo rasayan agle neuron ke dendrite par pahunchta hai aur wahan dobara bijli ban jaata hai.

📌 Synapse: Do neurons ke beech ki bahut chhoti khaali jagah. Yahan signal bijli → rasayan → bijli mein badalta hai.

💡 Faayda: Synapse ki wajah se signal sirf EK DISHA mein jaata hai — wapas nahi aa sakta. Isiliye sandesh mein gadbad nahi hoti.

⚡ Kitni Tez?

Nerve impulse lagbhag 100 metre per second tak ki raftaar se chal sakta hai — yani 360 km/h! Isiliye chot lagte hi turant dard mehsoos hota hai. Par ye phir bhi bijli ke taar (30 crore m/s) se bahut dheema hai — kyunki ye asli bijli nahi, rasayanik-bijli hai.

🧠 Yaad rakhne ki trick: Sensory = Sandesh andar laati hai. Motor = Muscle ko bhejti hai.

⚠️ Nervous system ki suraksha: Dimaag ko khopdi (skull) aur spinal cord ko reedh ki haddi bachati hai, aur dono ke beech tarl (CSF) gadde ka kaam karta hai. Isiliye cycle/bike chalate waqt helmet pehnna zaroori hai — ek chot poori zindagi badal sakti hai.
