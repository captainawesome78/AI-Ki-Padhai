Bina Dimaag Ke Bhi Samajhdaar 🌱

Paudhon ke paas na dimaag hai, na nerves, na maanspeshiyan. Phir bhi wo roshni ki taraf mudte hain, jadein paani dhoondh leti hain, aur chhui-mui chhoote hi sikud jaati hai! Ye sab hormones aur vriddhi (growth) se hota hai.

📌 Paudhon mein do tarah ki gatiyan hoti hain:
🎯 Tropic (dishik): uddipan ki disha ke hisaab se mudna — dheere, growth se.
⚡ Nastic (adishik): disha se koi matlab nahi — turant, jaise chhui-mui ka sikudna.

🎯 Tropic Movements — 4 Prakaar

NaamUddipanTana (stem)Jad (root)
PhototropismRoshniRoshni ki taraf (+)Roshni se door (−)
GeotropismGurutva (gravity)Upar, gravity ke ulta (−)Neeche, gravity ki taraf (+)
HydrotropismPaani—Paani ki taraf (+)
ThigmotropismChhuan / saharaSahare ke charon taraf lipatna (+)—
ChemotropismRasayanPollen tube ka ovule ki taraf badhna

🧠 Naam ka matlab yaad rakho: Photo = roshni, Geo = zameen/gravity, Hydro = paani, Thigmo = chhuan, Chemo = rasayan. Aur "tropism" = mudna.

🌞 Paudha Roshni Ki Taraf Kaise Mudta Hai?

🪟 Khidki Wale Gamle Ka Raaz

Khidki ke paas rakha gamla kuch dinon mein khidki ki taraf jhuk jaata hai. Kyun? Iska jawaab hai AUXIN naam ka hormone.

Jab roshni ek taraf se aati hai, toh auxin chhaanv wale (andhere) hisse mein jama ho jaata hai. Auxin cells ko lamba karta hai — toh andhere wale side ke cells zyada badh jaate hain, aur roshni wale side ke kam. Jab ek taraf zyada badhega toh tana apne aap doosri taraf jhuk jaayega — yani roshni ki taraf! Paudha "chal" nahi raha, wo ek taraf zyada badh raha hai.

Auxin chhaanv wali side mein jama hota hai aur cells ko lamba karta hai — isliye tana roshni ki taraf mudta hai.

🧪 Paudhon Ke Hormones

HormoneKaam
AuxinCells ko lamba karta hai; phototropism ka kaaran
GibberellinTane ki badhat; beej ka ankuran (germination)
CytokininCell division badhata hai; phal-phool ko taaza rakhta hai
Abscisic acid (ABA)Rokne wala hormone — badhat rokta hai, pattiyan girata hai, stomata band karta hai (isliye ise "stress hormone" kehte hain)

💡 Dhyan do: Pehle teen (auxin, gibberellin, cytokinin) badhat badhate hain, aur ABA badhat rokta hai. Board mein "kaunsa hormone growth inhibitor hai?" poocha jaata hai — jawaab abscisic acid.

🌿 Chhui-Mui (Touch-me-not) Ka Raaz

Chhui-mui ko chhoote hi uski pattiyan turant sikud jaati hain — ye nastic movement hai, tropic nahi! Isme growth shaamil nahi. Cells se paani achanak nikal jaata hai jisse wo pichak jaate hain aur patti mud jaati hai. Aur dhyan do — patti kis disha se chhui gayi, isse koi farak nahi padta. Isiliye ye "adishik" (non-directional) hai.

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharatiya kisan hormones ka istemal roz karte hain — kele ko pakane ke liye, angoor ko bada karne ke liye (gibberellin), aur nursery mein jadein ugane ke liye (auxin). Par dhyan rahe — phal pakane ke liye calcium carbide ka istemal Bharat mein kanoonan mana hai, kyunki wo sehat ke liye khatarnaak hai.

📖 Chapter Summary

✅ Control ke do tantra: nerves (tez, kam samay) aur hormones (dheeme, lambe samay)
✅ Neuron: dendrite → soma → axon → terminal; synapse par bijli→rasayan→bijli
✅ Reflex arc: Receptor → Sensory → Relay (spinal cord) → Motor → Effector
✅ Cerebrum = sochna, Cerebellum = santulan, Medulla = dhadkan-saans
✅ Insulin (shakkar), Thyroxine (metabolism, iodine chahiye), Adrenaline (fight/flight)
✅ Paudhe: phototropism/geotropism/hydrotropism; Auxin badhata hai, ABA rokta hai
