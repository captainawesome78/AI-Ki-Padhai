Shareer Ka Traffic Control 🎛️

Aapke shareer mein karodon cells hain, aur sab ko saath milkar kaam karna hai. Jab aap cricket ka catch pakadte ho — aankh gend dekhti hai, dimaag doori naapta hai, taangein daudti hain, haath sahi jagah pahunchte hain — sab kuch ek second se bhi kam mein! Ye tabhi ho sakta hai jab koi control aur coordination ka tantra ho.

📌 Uddipan (Stimulus): Aas-paas ka koi badlav jise shareer mehsoos karta hai — jaise roshni, aawaz, garmi, chhuan, gandh.
📌 Pratikriya (Response): Us badlav par shareer ki kriya — jaise haath hatana, aankh band karna, muh mein paani aana.

🍋 Nimbu Ka Naam Sunte Hi Muh Mein Paani

Sirf "khatta nimbu" padhne se hi aapke muh mein paani aa gaya na? Kamaal hai — na nimbu saamne hai, na aapne use chhua! Ye dikhata hai ki dimaag sirf abhi ke uddipan par nahi, balki yaadon par bhi pratikriya deta hai. Isi tarah pasandeeda khaane ki khushboo se hi bhookh lag jaati hai. Control system sirf machine nahi — wo seekhta bhi hai.

💡 Dono ki zaroorat kyun? Kuch kaam turant chahiye (kaante se haath hatana) — wahan nerves. Kuch kaam dheere aur lambe samay tak chahiye (bachche ka badhna) — wahan hormones. Ek tez daakiya hai, doosra dheema par door tak pahunchne wala.

👂 Receptors — Shareer Ke Sensor

Uddipan ko mehsoos karne wale khaas cells ko receptors kehte hain. Ye aksar hamari gyanendriyon (sense organs) mein hote hain:

🩺 Madhumeh (diabetes) ke mareezon mein kai baar haath-pair ke receptors kaam karna band kar dete hain — isse unhe chot ya garmi ka pata hi nahi chalta, aur ghaav badh jaata hai. Isiliye diabetes ke mareezon ko roz apne pair jaanchne ki salaah di jaati hai.

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharatiya yog aur pranayam sadiyon se swayat (autonomic) nervous system par kaam karte aaye hain — gehri saans lene se dhadkan dheemi hoti hai aur tanaav kam hota hai. Aaj ke vaigyanik prayogon ne bhi maana hai ki niyamit pranayam se rakt chaap (BP) aur tanaav ke hormone (cortisol) kam hote hain.
