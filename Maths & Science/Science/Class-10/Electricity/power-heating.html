Bijli Garmi Kyun Banti Hai? ♨️

Heater garam hota hai. Iron garam hota hai. Phone charge karte waqt charger halka garam ho jaata hai. Bulb chhoo lo toh haath jal jaata hai. Kyun?

Yaad karo: electrons taar mein aage badhte waqt atoms se takraate hain. Har takkar mein wo thodi energy khote hain — aur wo energy garmi ban jaati hai. Isi ko heating effect of electric current kehte hain.

📌 Joule ka heating ka niyam:

H = I² R t

Yani paida hui garmi:
🔹 Current ke varg ke ratio (anupaat) mein (I²) — ye sabse zaroori hai!
🔹 Resistance ke seedhe ratio mein (R)
🔹 Samay ke seedhe ratio mein (t)

H joule mein, I ampere mein, R ohm mein, t SECOND mein.

⚡ I ka VARG — isliye current dugna karna 4 guna garmi deta hai

H = I²Rt mein I ka varg hai. Iska matlab:

🔹 Current dugna karo → garmi 4 guna
🔹 Current teen guna → garmi 9 guna

Isiliye bijli ke lambe taaron mein current ko jitna ho sake kam rakha jaata hai — aur utni hi power bhejne ke liye voltage bahut zyada kar diya jaata hai (P = VI).

Aur isiliye ek socket par zyada upkaran lagane par taar itni jaldi garam ho jaata hai: current thoda badha, par garmi varg ke hisaab se badhi.

💡 Electric Power — Teen Roop

📌 Power = ek second mein kharch hui energy.

P = V I
Aur V = IR lagakar do aur roop nikal aate hain:
P = I² R   aur   P = V² / R

Ikai: watt (W).   1 watt = 1 volt × 1 ampere

🎯 Kaunsa roop kab lagaao — ye pehchaan lo:
🔹 V aur I diya hai → P = VI
🔹 I aur R diya hai → P = I²R
🔹 V aur R diya hai → P = V²/R

Teeno ek hi cheez hain — bas jo diya hai uske hisaab se chuno. Isse aadha kaam bach jaata hai.

📝 Sawaal 1 — Bulb Ka Poora Hisaab

Ek 60 W ka bulb 220 V par chalta hai. Uska current aur resistance nikaalo.

Current: P = VI → I = P/V
I = 60/220 = 0.273 A

Resistance: P = V²/R → R = V²/P
R = (220 × 220) / 60 = 48400/60 = 806.67 Ω

✅ Teeno formule se jaanch:
P = VI = 220 × 0.273 = 60 W ✓
P = V²/R = 48400/806.67 = 60 W ✓
P = I²R = (0.273)² × 806.67 = 60 W ✓
Teeno mel khaate hain — jawab pakka sahi hai.

📝 Sawaal 2 — Heater

Ek 1000 W ka heater 220 V par chalta hai. Current aur resistance?

Hal: I = P/V = 1000/220 = 4.55 A
R = V²/P = 48400/1000 = 48.4 Ω

💡 Bulb ke saath tulna karo: heater ka resistance bahut kam (48 Ω vs 807 Ω) hai, isliye usme current bahut zyada (4.55 A vs 0.27 A) bahta hai — aur isiliye wo itni garmi deta hai.

⚠️ Aur isiliye heater ke liye mota taar aur zyada ampere ka fuse chahiye.

📝 Sawaal 3 — Garmi Nikaalo

Ek 10 Ω ke resistor mein 5 A current 1 minute bahta hai. Kitni garmi bani?

Step 1 — samay second mein: t = 60 s

Step 2: H = I²Rt = (5)² × 10 × 60
H = 25 × 10 × 60 = 15000 J = 15 kJ

💰 Bijli Ka Bill — kWh Ka Khel

Bijli ka bill watt ka nahi, energy ka aata hai. Aur wo energy joule mein nahi naapi jaati (joule bahut chhoti ikai hai) — balki kilowatt-hour (kWh) mein.

📌 1 kWh = 1 kilowatt ka upkaran 1 ghante chalane par kharch hui energy.
Ise hi "1 unit" kehte hain — wahi jo aapke bijli ke bill par likha hota hai.

Units (kWh) = (Watt × Ghante) / 1000

Bill = Units × rate per unit

Aur joule mein: 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J

📝 Sawaal 4 — Heater Ka Mahine Ka Kharcha

Ek 1000 W ka heater roz 2 ghante chalta hai. Ek mahine (30 din) mein kitne units? Aur ₹6 per unit ke hisaab se bill kitna?

Step 1 — kul ghante: 2 × 30 = 60 ghante

Step 2 — units:
Units = (1000 × 60) / 1000 = 60 units

Step 3 — bill:
Bill = 60 × 6 = ₹360

😲 Sirf ek heater, roz do ghante — aur mahine ka ₹360!

📝 Sawaal 5 — Poore Ghar Ka Bill (asli zindagi wala)

Ek ghar mein ye cheezein hain. Mahine ka bill nikaalo (30 din, ₹6 per unit):

UpkaranPowerRozUnits/mahina
LED bulb9 W6 h(9×6×30)/1000 = 1.6
Pankha75 W8 h(75×8×30)/1000 = 18
Fridge150 W24 h(150×24×30)/1000 = 108
TV100 W4 h(100×4×30)/1000 = 12
Heater1500 W1 h(1500×1×30)/1000 = 45
KUL184.6 units

Bill = 184.6 × 6 = ₹1,108

💡 Do cheezein gaur karo:
🔹 Fridge sabse zyada bijli khaata hai (108 units) — power kam hai par wo 24 ghante chalta hai!
🔹 Heater sirf 1 ghanta chalta hai, phir bhi 45 units — kyunki uski power bahut zyada hai.

Yani bill = power × samay. Dono maayne rakhte hain.

💡 Bijli bachaane ke asli tareeke — ab aap samajh sakte ho kyun:
🔹 LED use karo — ek 9 W ka LED utni hi roshni deta hai jitna 60 W ka purana bulb. Yani 85% bachat.
🔹 Heater, geyser, iron ka samay kam karo — inki power sabse zyada hai
🔹 Fridge ka darwaza baar-baar mat kholo — usse compressor zyada chalta hai
🔹 5-star rating wale upkaran lo — wo kam watt mein wahi kaam karte hain

🛡️ Fuse — Heating Effect Ka Sabse Achha Istemaal

Heating effect aksar nuksaandeh hota hai. Par ek jagah wo jaan bachata hai — fuse mein.

📌 Fuse ek patla taar hota hai jiska melting point kam hota hai (aksar tin aur lead ki alloy).

Wo hamesha SERIES mein lagta hai — taaki poora current usse guzre.

Jab current zaroorat se zyada ho jaata hai, toh H = I²Rt ke hisaab se fuse taar itna garam ho jaata hai ki wo pighal kar toot jaata hai — aur poora circuit band ho jaata hai.

Ek chhota sa taar jaan-boojh kar kurbaan hota hai, taaki poora ghar aur usme rehne wale log bach jaayein.

Fuse H = I²Rt par hi chalta hai. Current zyada hote hi wo sabse pehle garam hokar pighalta hai aur circuit tod deta hai.
🔢 Fuse Ka Rating Kaise Chunte Hain

Ek kamre mein ye cheezein chalti hain (220 V par):
🔹 Do bulb 60 W ke = 120 W
🔹 Ek pankha 75 W
🔹 Ek TV 100 W
Kul power = 295 W

Kul current: I = P/V = 295/220 = 1.34 A

Toh yahan 5 A ka fuse theek rahega — thoda upar, taaki saamanya kaam mein bina wajah na ude, par khatre mein zaroor ude.

⚠️ Agar koi yahan 30 A ka fuse laga de, toh wo kabhi nahi udega — aur taar pighal kar aag lag jaayegi. Bada fuse = zyada suraksha nahi, balki kam suraksha.

⚠️ Kabhi bhi fuse ki jagah taanba ya aluminium ka taar mat lagaao. Gaon-shehar dono mein log fuse ud jaane par usme mota taar daal dete hain taaki "baar-baar na ude". Ye bahut khatarnaak hai.

Kyun? Kyunki mota taar pighlega hi nahi — wo poora current guzarne dega. Aur phir jo pighlega wo hoga ghar ki wiring, deewar ke andar, jahan aapko dikhega bhi nahi — jab tak aag na lag jaaye.

Fuse baar-baar udta hai iska matlab hai ki kuch galat hai — ya toh load zyada hai, ya kahin short circuit hai. Uska hal fuse badalna nahi, electrician bulana hai.

Aaj kal fuse ki jagah MCB (Miniature Circuit Breaker) lagta hai — wo pighalta nahi, bas switch ki tarah gir jaata hai aur dobara uthaya ja sakta hai.

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat ka "UJALA" abhiyaan duniya ka sabse bada LED baantne wala programme hai. 2015 se ab tak 36 crore se zyada LED bulb baante ja chuke hain.

Physics kya hai? Ek purana bulb 60 W ka tha aur uski lagbhag 95% energy garmi mein chali jaati thi (isiliye wo itna garam hota tha) — sirf 5% roshni banti thi. Ek LED 9 W mein utni hi roshni deta hai, kyunki wo bahut kam garmi banata hai.

Ek ghar ke liye ye chhoti bachat lagti hai. Par 36 crore bulb par? Sarkaari aankdon ke mutabik isse har saal lagbhag 4,800 crore units bijli bach rahi hai — yani kai bade bijli-ghar banane ki zaroorat hi khatam ho gayi, aur crore ton CO₂ kam nikla.

P = VI aur Units = Watt × Ghante / 1000 — do chhote formule, aur unse poore desh ki bijli ki tasveer badal gayi.
