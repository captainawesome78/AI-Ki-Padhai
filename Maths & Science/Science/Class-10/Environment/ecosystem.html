Ek Talab Se Shuruaat 💧

Apne gaon ya shehar ke kisi talab ke paas khade ho jao aur dhyaan se dekho.

Paani hai, mitti hai, dhoop pad rahi hai. Paani mein kaai (algae) hai, kuch machhliyan hain, kinare par mendhak baitha hai, upar bagula udd raha hai. Aur jo aapko dikh nahi raha — paani aur mitti mein karodon bacteria aur fungi hain.

Ye sab alag-alag cheezein nahi hain. Ye sab ek doosre par nirbhar hain. Isi poore jode ko ecosystem kehte hain.

📌 Ecosystem = kisi jagah ke saare jeev + wahan ka poora bejaan maahol + un dono ka aapsi rishta.

Do hisse hote hain:
🌱 Biotic (sajeev) — paudhe, jaanwar, bacteria, fungi
🪨 Abiotic (nirjeev) — dhoop, hawa, paani, mitti, taapmaan

🏫 Aapka school bhi ek ecosystem hai

Ecosystem bahut bada bhi ho sakta hai aur bahut chhota bhi:

🌊 Bahut bade: samundar, jungle, registan
🏞️ Madhyam: ek talab, ek khet, ek nadi
🪴 Chhote: ek gamla, ek aquarium, ek pedh
🔬 Bahut chhote: ek sadi hui lakdi ka tukda!

School ke bageeche mein bhi ghaas, keede, chidiya, mitti aur dhoop — sab ek doosre par nirbhar hain. Wo bhi ek poora ecosystem hai.

📌 Do prakaar ke ecosystem:

🌿 Prakritik (natural) — jo apne aap bane: jungle, samundar, nadi, talab, registan
🏡 Manav-nirmit (artificial) — jo insaan ne banaye: khet, bageecha, aquarium, park

⚠️ Farak samjho: prakritik ecosystem khud sambhal lete hain. Manav-nirmit ecosystem ko lagataar dekhbhaal chahiye — paani dena, khaad daalna, keede hataana. Chhod do toh wo bikhar jaate hain.

🎭 Teen Kirdaar — Har Ecosystem Mein

Har ecosystem mein jeevon ke teen kaam hote hain. Teeno mein se koi ek bhi hat jaaye toh poora system ruk jaata hai.

Ecosystem ke teen kirdaar. Dhyaan do: poshak element GHOOMTE hain, par energy sirf ek taraf bahti hai — isliye sooraj ki roshni har roz zaroori hai.
KirdaarKaunKya karta hai
Producer
(utpaadak)		Hare paudhe, kaai (algae), kuch bacteriaPhotosynthesis se khud apna khaana banate hain. Poore ecosystem ki energy inhi se aati hai.
Consumer
(upbhokta)		Saare jaanwar aur insaanKhud khaana nahi bana sakte — doosron ko khaate hain.
Decomposer
(apghatak)		Bacteria aur fungiMare hue jeev aur kachre ko todkar poshak element mitti mein wapas bhejte hain.

📌 Consumers ke chaar prakaar:
🐄 Herbivores (shaakahari) — sirf paudhe khaate hain: gaay, hiran, khargosh, tiddi
🐅 Carnivores (maansahari) — sirf maans khaate hain: sher, saanp, bagula
🐻 Omnivores (sarvahari) — dono khaate hain: insaan, kauwa, bhaalu, kutta
🦅 Scavengers / Parasites — mara hua maans khaane wale (giddh, kauwa), ya doosron par jeene wale (joon, kirmi)

🍄 Decomposers — Sabse Zaroori Aur Sabse Nazarandaz

Decomposers ko koi pasand nahi karta. Fungi, bacteria, sadan — sab gandagi lagti hai. Par ek soch kar dekho:

🍂 Agar Decomposers Na Hote To?

Socho ki kal se duniya ke saare bacteria aur fungi gaayab ho jaayein.

🍂 Pedh se gire hue patte kabhi nahi galenge. Saal-dar-saal wo jamte jaayenge.
🐘 Mare hue jaanwar waise ke waise pade rahenge.
💩 Gobar aur kachra kabhi khatam nahi hoga.

Aur sabse buri baat: har mare hue jeev ke andar jo nitrogen, phosphorus aur carbon band hai, wo hamesha ke liye band reh jaayega. Mitti mein wapas nahi aayega.

Nateeja? Kuch hi saalon mein mitti bilkul bekaar ho jaayegi, paudhe ugna band kar denge — aur unke bina saare jaanwar bhookhe mar jaayenge.

Yani decomposers na hote toh prithvi par jeevan hi ruk jaata. Aur wo har roz, bina kisi ko dikhe, ye kaam kar rahe hain.

♻️ Poshak element GHOOMTE hain, energy NAHI.

Ye is chapter ka sabse zaroori vichaar hai:

🔄 Nitrogen, carbon, paani — ye ghoomte rehte hain. Mitti se paudhe mein, paudhe se jaanwar mein, jaanwar se decomposers dwara wapas mitti mein. Ek chakra (cycle).

➡️ Energy — ye ghoomti nahi. Sooraj se aati hai, ek taraf bahti hai, aur har step par garmi bankar hamesha ke liye chali jaati hai.

Isiliye ecosystem ko har roz nayi dhoop chahiye — par nayi mitti nahi chahiye.

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat ke gaon mein sadiyon se gobar ka istemaal hota aaya hai — aur wo isi decomposer wale vigyan par chalta hai. Gobar ko gaddhe mein daal do, bacteria use tod dete hain, aur kuch mahine mein wo khaad (compost) ban jaata hai jo khet ko poshak element wapas de deti hai.

Aaj isi ko vermicompost (kenchue se banne wali khaad) aur gobar gas plant ke roop mein aage badhaya gaya hai — jisse gaon mein khaana pakane ki gas bhi milti hai aur bachi hui slurry se khaad bhi.

Bharat sarkaar ka "GOBARdhan" yojana isi par hai. Yani jise hum "gandagi" kehte hain, wo asal mein ek poora chakra hai jo hazaron saal se chal raha hai.
