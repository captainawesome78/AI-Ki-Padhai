Energy Ka Bahav Aur 10% Ka Niyam 📉

Home Class 10 Science Chapter 13

Energy Ek Hi Taraf Bahti Hai ➡️

Saari energy sooraj se aati hai. Paudhe use photosynthesis se pakadte hain, phir wo food chain mein upar chadhti hai.

Par ek bahut zaroori baat: energy kabhi wapas nahi aati. Wo ek hi disha mein bahti hai — aur har step par uska bada hissa garmi bankar hamesha ke liye chala jaata hai.

📌 10% ka niyam (Lindeman's law):

Ek trophic level ki energy ka sirf 10% agle level tak pahunchta hai.
Baaki 90% khatam ho jaata hai.

Ye 90% jaata kahan hai?
🔥 Saans lene (respiration) mein — sabse bada hissa
🏃 Chalne, daudne, shikaar karne mein
🌡️ Shareer ka taapmaan banaye rakhne mein
💩 Mal-mutra mein jo bina pache nikal jaata hai
🦴 Haddi, baal, sing — jo khaaya hi nahi jaata

Energy pyramid. Har step par 90% garmi bankar ud jaata hai — isiliye pyramid upar jaate-jaate itna patla ho jaata hai.

🧮 Hal Kiye Hue Sawaal

📝 Sawaal 1 — Energy Ka Hisaab

Ek ecosystem mein producers ke paas 10,000 J energy hai. Har agle level par kitni bachegi?

Hal — har baar 10% lo:
Trophic 1 (producers) = 10,000 J
Trophic 2 = 10,000 × 10/100 = 1,000 J
Trophic 3 = 1,000 × 10/100 = 100 J
Trophic 4 = 100 × 10/100 = 10 J
Trophic 5 = 10 × 10/100 = 1 J

😲 Shuru mein 10,000 J tha, aur paanchve level par sirf 1 J — yani 0.01%!

📝 Sawaal 2 — Kitni Ghaas Chahiye?

1 kg sher banane ke liye kitni ghaas chahiye? (chain: ghaas → hiran → sher)

Ulta chalo:
1 kg sher ke liye chahiye 10 kg hiran (kyunki sirf 10% pahunchta hai)
10 kg hiran ke liye chahiye 100 kg ghaas

✅ Jawab: 100 kg ghaas se sirf 1 kg sher banta hai!

Isiliye kisi bhi jungle mein ghaas sabse zyada, hiran usse kam, aur sher sabse kam hote hain. Ye koi ittefaq nahi — ye 10% ke niyam ka seedha nateeja hai.

📝 Practice — Khud Karo

(a) Producers ke paas 5,000 J hai. Trophic level 3 par kitni energy hogi?
Jawab: 5000 → 500 → 50 J

(b) 1000 kg ghaas se kitne kg top carnivore (level 4) ban sakta hai?
Jawab: 1000 → 100 → 10 → 1 kg

(c) Agar level 2 par 200 J hai, toh level 1 par kitna tha?
Jawab: ulta chalo — 200 × 10 = 2000 J

❓ Food Chain Chhoti Kyun Hoti Hai?

🎯 Ab jawab bilkul saaf hai. Har level par 90% energy khatam ho jaati hai. 4-5 level ke baad itni kam energy bachti hai ki wo kisi jeev ko zinda rakhne ke liye kaafi hi nahi hoti.

Isiliye prakriti mein food chain aksar 3 se 5 level ki hi hoti hai — usse lambi lagbhag kabhi nahi.

Aur isiliye koi jaanwar sher ko nahi khaata — sher ke upar ek aur level ke liye energy bachti hi nahi. Sher jaise jaanwar ko apex predator kehte hain.

🌾 Shaakahari khaana kam zameen kyun leta hai?

10% ke niyam ka ek bahut bada asli-duniya wala nateeja hai.

Agar aap seedhe gehun khaate ho, toh aap trophic level 2 par ho — aur aapko producers ki energy ka 10% mil raha hai.

Par agar aap maans khaate ho, toh us jaanwar ne pehle anaaj khaaya, phir aap use khaa rahe ho — aap level 3 par ho, aur aapko sirf 1% mil raha hai.

Yani ek kilo maans banane ke liye kai kilo anaaj aur bahut saara paani lagta hai. Isiliye kaha jaata hai ki utni hi zameen se bahut zyada log khilaye ja sakte hain agar wo seedhe anaaj khaayein.

Ye kisi ko kya khaana chahiye iska faisla nahi hai — ye bas 10% ke niyam ka seedha ganit hai, jo aaj duniya bhar mein khaadya suraksha (food security) ki charcha ka hissa hai.

📌 Do zaroori nateeje yaad rakho:

1️⃣ Energy ka bahav EK TARFA (unidirectional) hai. Sooraj → producers → consumers → garmi. Wapas kabhi nahi.

2️⃣ Har level par jeevon ki ginti aur bhaar ghatta hai. Isiliye energy pyramid hamesha seedha (upright) hota hai — neeche chauda, upar patla. Kabhi ulta nahi.

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat ke Project Tiger mein yahi ganit sabse zaroori hai. Ek sher ko zinda rehne ke liye chahiye bahut saare hiran; aur un hiran ko chahiye bahut badi ghaas ki zameen.

Isiliye ek sher ko lagbhag 20-40 varg kilometre ka apna ilaaka chahiye hota hai. Aur isiliye tiger reserve itne bade banaye jaate hain — sirf sher bachaane se kaam nahi chalta, poora pyramid bachana padta hai: ghaas, hiran, paani, sab.

1973 mein Project Tiger shuru hua tha jab bharat mein sirf lagbhag 1,800 sher bache the. Aaj wo ginti 3,600 se zyada hai — duniya ke lagbhag 70% jangli sher bharat mein hain.

Aur ek sundar baat: sher bachaane ke chakkar mein jo bade jungle bache, unse hazaron doosri prajaatiyan, nadiyon ke srot aur crore logon ki hawa bhi bach gayi. Isiliye sher ko "umbrella species (prajaati)" kehte hain.
