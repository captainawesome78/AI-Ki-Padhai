Kaun Kise Khaata Hai? 🍽️

Ghaas ko hiran khaata hai. Hiran ko sher khaata hai. Isi khaane-khaane ki kadi ko food chain kehte hain.

📌 Food chain = jeevon ki wo kadi jisme har jeev agle ka khaana banta hai — aur isi tarah energy ek se doosre tak pahunchti hai.

Har kadi (step) ko trophic level (nutrition star) kehte hain.

⚠️ Food chain hamesha producer se shuru hoti hai — kyunki wahi ek hai jo sooraj se energy le sakta hai.

Ek food chain. Teer hamesha KHAANE WALE ki taraf hota hai — kyunki energy usi disha mein ja rahi hai. Ye exam mein bahut poocha jaata hai!

⚠️ Sabse aam galti — teer ki disha:

Teer hamesha khaaye jaane wale se khaane wale ki taraf jaata hai.
Ghaas → Hiran → Sher

Log ulta likh dete hain (sher → hiran) kyunki lagta hai "sher hiran ko khaata hai". Par teer khaane ki disha nahi, ENERGY ke bahne ki disha dikhata hai. Aur energy ghaas se hiran mein jaati hai.

💡 Dhyaan do: har chain producer se shuru hoti hai, aur har chain mein sirf 3 se 5 level hote hain. Agle lesson mein pata chalega ki kyun zyada nahi ho sakte.

🕸️ Food Web — Asli Jungle Ka Sach

Ab ek sawaal: kya sher sirf hiran khaata hai? Aur kya hiran ko sirf sher khaata hai?

Bilkul nahi! Sher jungli suar bhi khaata hai, nilgai bhi. Aur hiran ko tendua bhi khaata hai, jungli kutte bhi. Asli prakriti mein seedhi chain nahi, ek poora JAAL hota hai.

📌 Food web = bahut saari food chains jo aapas mein judi hoti hain.

Har jeev aksar kai cheezein khaata hai, aur use bhi kai khaate hain. Isliye prakriti mein ek jaal banta hai.

Ek food web. Dekho — saanp ko chooha bhi milta hai aur mendhak bhi. Isliye ek shikaar khatam ho jaaye toh wo bhookha nahi marta.

🎯 Food web itna zaroori kyun hai?

Kyunki wo ecosystem ko mazboot banata hai.

🔗 Seedhi chain mein: agar hiran khatam ho gaye, toh sher bhookha mar jaayega — poori chain toot gayi.

🕸️ Web mein: agar chooha khatam ho gaya, toh saanp mendhak khaakar zinda reh sakta hai. Web mein vikalp (options) hote hain.

Isiliye jitni zyada variation (biodiversity), utna mazboot ecosystem.

🐍 Ek jeev hatao — poora system hil jaata hai

Maan lo kisi gaon mein log saanp maar dete hain, ye sochkar ki wo khatarnaak hain.

Kya hoga? Saanp choohe khaate the. Ab koi khaane wala nahi bacha — choohon ki ginti tezi se badhegi.

Aur choohe kya karenge? Khet ka anaaj khaayenge aur gharon mein ghusenge. Fasal barbaad, aur bimariyan alag.

Yani saanp ko maar kar gaon ne apna hi nuksaan kar liya. Isiliye kehte hain ki saanp kisan ka dost hai — aur ye sirf kahaawat nahi, seedha ecology ka niyam hai.

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat mein iska sabse bada aur dukhad udaharan hai — giddh (vultures).

1990 ke dashak tak bharat mein karodon giddh the. Wo mare hue jaanwaron ka maans khaakar prakritik safai ka kaam karte the — ek tarah ke scavenger.

Phir pashuon ko ek dard ki dawa diclofenac di jaane lagi. Jab giddh un mare hue pashuon ko khaate, toh wo dawa unke gurde kharab kar deti. Nateeja: lagbhag 99% giddh khatam ho gaye — duniya ki sabse tez pakshi giravat.

Asar kya hua? Mare hue pashu ab pade rehne lage, jisse aawara kutton ki ginti bahut badh gayi — aur uske saath rabies ke maamle bhi.

Bharat ne 2006 mein diclofenac ke pashu istemaal par rok laga di, aur ab vulture breeding centre chal rahe hain. Par ek chhoti si dawa se ek poore web ko kitna nuksaan hua — ye humesha yaad rakhne laayak hai.
