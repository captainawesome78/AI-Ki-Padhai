Do Chehron Wali Gas 🎭

Ozone ek ajeeb gas hai — wo ek hi samay par zeher bhi hai aur raksha kavach bhi. Farak sirf itna hai ki wo kahan hai.

📌 Ozone = O₃ — oxygen ke teen parmaanu.
Jo hawa hum saans lete hain wo O₂ hai — sirf do parmaanu.

😷 Zameen ke paas (troposphere): ozone ek haanikaarak pradushak hai. Wo phephdon ko nuksaan pahunchati hai aur saans ki takleef badhati hai.

🛡️ Bahut upar (stratosphere, lagbhag 15-35 km): wahi ozone ek dhaal ka kaam karti hai jo sooraj ki khatarnaak UV rays ko rok leti hai.

Isiliye kehte hain: "Ozone — upar achhi, neeche buri."

🌫️ Ozone layer kitni patli hai?

"Layer" sunkar lagta hai koi moti chaadar hogi. Par sach ye hai ki agar poori ozone layer ko daba kar zameen par bichha diya jaaye, toh wo sirf 3 millimetre moti hogi — do sikkon jitni!

Aur wahi 3 mm poori prithvi ke jeevan ko UV se bacha rahi hai. Isiliye usme thoda sa nuksaan bhi itni badi baat ban jaata hai.

⚗️ Ozone Banti Kaise Hai

📌 Bahut upar, sooraj ki tez UV rays oxygen ke anu (O₂) ko tod deti hain:

O₂  --UV-->  O + O

Phir ye akela oxygen parmaanu kisi doosre O₂ se jud jaata hai:

O + O₂  →  O₃ (ozone)

Aur ozone khud bhi UV pakad kar wapas toot jaati hai. Yani wahan upar ozone lagataar banti aur tootti rehti hai — aur isi tod-phod mein wo UV ki energy khaa jaati hai. Yahi hume bachata hai.

Saabut ozone layer UV ko rok leti hai. Par CFC se toote hisse se UV seedhe zameen tak pahunch jaati hai.

💥 CFC — Ek Chhoti Si Gas Ne Kya Kar Diya

📌 CFC (Chlorofluorocarbon) — ek gas jo purane fridge, AC, aur spray ki bottalon (aerosol) mein istemaal hoti thi.

CFC ko "surakshit" maana jaata tha kyunki wo kisi cheez se react nahi karti — zeher nahi, aag nahi pakadti. Aur yahi uski sabse badi dikkat nikli.

Kyunki wo tootti nahi, isliye wo saalon tak upar chadhti rehti hai — aakhir stratosphere tak. Wahan tez UV use tod deti hai aur usme se chlorine (Cl) nikal aata hai.

⚠️ Aur wo chlorine ozone ko todta hai — par khud khatam nahi hota. Ek hi chlorine parmaanu hazaron ozone ke anu tod sakta hai, aur wahan dashkon tak zinda rehta hai.

☀️ UV rays zameen tak pahunchne par kya nuksaan:
🧑 Insaanon mein — skin cancer, motiyabind (cataract), aur rog-pratirodhak kshamata (immunity) kamzor
🌾 Paudhon mein — photosynthesis kam, isliye fasal kam
🌊 Samundar mein — plankton mar jaate hain, jo poori samudri food chain ka aadhaar hain

🌍 Duniya Ne Ek Baar Sach Mein Mil Kar Kaam Kiya

1985 mein vaigyanikon ne Antarctica ke upar ozone layer mein ek bahut bada "chhed" (hole) khoja. (Wo asal mein chhed nahi, bahut patla hua hissa tha.)

Ye khabar itni darawni thi ki duniya ne wo kar dikhaya jo aaj tak dobara nahi ho paaya — sab desh ek saath maan gaye.

1987 mein Montreal Protocol par dastakhat hue, jisme CFC ko dheere-dheere poori tarah band karne ka waada kiya gaya. Ye duniya ki pehli sandhi hai jise har ek desh ne apnaaya — 198 mein se 198.

Nateeja? Kaam kar gaya! Ozone layer dheere-dheere theek ho rahi hai. Vaigyanikon ka anumaan hai ki 2050-2070 tak wo lagbhag 1980 wali haalat mein wapas aa jaayegi.

Sabak: paryavaran ki samasyaayein hal ho sakti hain — agar duniya vigyan ki baat sune aur saath mile. Ozone iska sabse achha saboot hai.

⚠️ Ozone hole aur Global Warming ek cheez NAHI hain! Ye exam mein bahut poocha jaata hai:

🕳️ Ozone depletion — CFC se hota hai. Isse UV rays zameen tak pahunchti hain. Ilaaj: CFC band karo.

🌡️ Global warming — CO₂ aur greenhouse gases se hota hai. Isse prithvi ka taapmaan badhta hai. Ilaaj: CO₂ kam karo.

Dono alag samasyaayein hain, alag gases se, aur alag ilaaj chahiye.

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat ne 1992 mein Montreal Protocol par dastakhat kiye, aur apna waada samay se pehle poora kiya — bharat ne CFC ka istemaal 2010 tak poori tarah band kar diya.

Isiliye aaj aap jo naya fridge ya AC khareedte ho, usmein CFC nahi hoti. Uspar aksar "CFC-free" ya "Ozone friendly" likha hota hai — ghar ja kar apne fridge par dekhna!

Aur bharat ne 2021 mein Kigali Amendment bhi apnaaya, jisme CFC ke baad aayi HFC gases ko bhi kam kiya ja raha hai (wo ozone ko nuksaan nahi karti, par global warming badhati hain).

Har saal 16 September ko duniya "World Ozone Day" manati hai — usi din 1987 mein Montreal Protocol par dastakhat hue the.
