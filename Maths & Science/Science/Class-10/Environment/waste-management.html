Kachra "Chala Jaata Hai" — Kahan? 🤔

Aap kachra dustbin mein daalte ho. Gaadi aati hai, le jaati hai. Aur baat khatam.

Par wo kachra gaayab nahi hota. Wo kisi na kisi jagah jaata hai — aksar shehar ke bahar ek bade kachre ke pahaad (landfill) par. Aur wahan wo saalon, kabhi-kabhi sadiyon tak pada rehta hai.

📌 Kachra do tarah ka hota hai — aur yahi is poore lesson ki jad hai:

🌱 Biodegradable (jaiv nishnaneey) — jise bacteria aur fungi tod sakte hain. Ye kuch dinon ya mahinon mein mitti mein mil jaata hai.

🧴 Non-biodegradable (ajaiv nishnaneey) — jise decomposers tod NAHI sakte. Ye saalon-sadiyon tak waisa ka waisa pada rehta hai.

⏳ Kis cheez ko galne mein kitna samay lagta hai?

🍌 Kele ka chhilka — 2 se 5 hafte
📄 Kaagaz — 2 se 5 mahine
🍎 Seb ka gudda — 2 mahine
👕 Sooti kapda — 1 se 5 mahine
🥫 Tin ka dabba — 50 se 100 saal
🥤 Plastic ki botal — 450 saal
🛍️ Plastic ki thaili — 10 se 1000 saal (halaat par nirbhar)
🍾 Kaanch ki botal — lagbhag kabhi nahi (10 lakh saal se zyada)

😲 Iska matlab: aaj aapne jo plastic ki botal phenki, wo aapke par-par-pote ke zamaane tak bhi zameen mein padi hogi. Aur duniya mein bana har plastic ka tukda (kuch jale hue ko chhodkar) aaj bhi kahin na kahin maujood hai.

♻️ 4 R — sahi kram mein (kram bahut zaroori hai!):

1️⃣ REFUSE — jiski zaroorat nahi, use lena hi mat. (Sabse achha tareeka — kachra bana hi nahi.)
2️⃣ REDUCE — kam istemaal karo. Kam kharido, kam phenko.
3️⃣ REUSE — dobara istemaal karo. Kaanch ki botal mein masala bhar lo.
4️⃣ RECYCLE — jab aur kuch na bache, tab galaakar nayi cheez banao.

⚠️ Log seedhe recycle par chale jaate hain — par wo sabse aakhri vikalp hai, kyunki usme bhi bahut energy lagti hai. Sabse achha toh kachra banne hi na dena hai.

Bharat ke Solid Waste Management Rules ke hisaab se teen dabbe. Segregation (prathakkaran) ghar se shuru hota hai — aur wahi sabse zaroori kadam hai.

🙋 Aur Aap Kya Kar Sakte Ho

🎯 Ye lesson ka sabse zaroori hissa hai. "Sarkaar kuch kare" kehna aasaan hai — par segregation ghar se hi shuru hota hai. Agar sab kachra ek saath mila diya jaaye, toh usme se kuch bhi recycle karna lagbhag namumkin ho jaata hai.

Aaj se ye 7 cheezein kar sakte ho:

1️⃣ Ghar mein do dabbe rakho — ek geela, ek sookha
2️⃣ Bazaar jaate waqt kapde ka thaila le jao
3️⃣ Apni paani ki botal le kar chalo — plastic wali mat khareedo
4️⃣ Sabzi ke chhilkon se gamle mein khaad banao
5️⃣ Purani kitaabein aur kapde kisi ko de do, phenko mat
6️⃣ Purani battery aur e-waste kabhi aam kachre mein mat daalo
7️⃣ Ghar mein sabko ye batao — ek vyakti ke badalne se zyada asar tab hota hai jab poora ghar badle

🪴 Ghar Par Compost — Bahut Aasaan Hai

Chahiye: ek purana gamla ya balti (neeche chhota chhed karo), sabzi ke chhilke, sookhe patte, thodi mitti.

Tareeka:
1️⃣ Neeche mitti ki ek parat daalo
2️⃣ Uspar geela kachra (chhilke, bacha khaana) daalo
3️⃣ Uspar sookhe patte ya kaagaz ki parat — ye badbu rokte hain
4️⃣ Aise parat-dar-parat bharte jao, aur har 3-4 din mein halka sa mila do
5️⃣ 6-8 hafte mein wo kaali, bhurbhuri khaad ban jaayegi

⚠️ Isme mat daalo: maans, doodh ki cheezein, tel — inse badbu aur keede aate hain.

💡 Ye khaad gamlon mein daalo — aur dekho paudhe kitne achhe se badhte hain. Aap seedhe decomposers ka kaam apne ghar par karwa rahe ho!

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat har din lagbhag 1.5 lakh ton se zyada thos kachra paida karta hai — aur uska bada hissa aaj bhi bina alag kiye landfill par pahunch jaata hai.

Delhi ka Ghazipur landfill itna ooncha ho gaya hai ki wo lagbhag Qutub Minar jitna hai — aur usme kai baar aag lag chuki hai.

Par ummeed ki kahaniyan bhi hain:
🏆 Indore lagataar kai saal se bharat ka sabse saaf shehar raha hai — aur uski sabse badi wajah yahi hai ki wahan ghar-ghar se alag-alag kachra uthaya jaata hai.
🌿 Alappuzha (Kerala) ne kachre ko ghar par hi compost karke apni landfill ki zaroorat lagbhag khatam kar di.
🚫 Bharat ne 2022 mein kai single-use plastic cheezon par rok laga di.

Aur ek kahani jo sabse purani hai: Bishnoi samaj (Rajasthan) sadiyon se pedhon aur jaanwaron ki raksha karta aaya hai. 1730 mein Amrita Devi aur 363 logon ne khejri ke pedh bachaane ke liye apni jaan de di thi. Isi soch se aage chalkar 1970 ke dashak mein Chipko Andolan nikla, jisme log pedhon se chipak gaye taaki unhe kaata na ja sake.

Yani paryavaran bachaane ki soch bharat ke liye nayi nahi — bahut purani hai.
