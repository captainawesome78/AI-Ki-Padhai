Ek Purana Sawaal 🤔

Ek pehalwaan zindagi bhar mehnat karke bahut mazboot muscles banata hai. Kya uska beta paida hote hi thoda zyada mazboot hoga? Ek aadmi zindagi bhar mehnat se angrezi seekhta hai — kya uska bachcha thodi angrezi jaanta hua paida hoga?

Aaj hum sab jaante hain ki jawab nahi hai. Par 200 saal pehle duniya ke sabse bade vaigyanikon mein se ek ne kaha tha ki jawab haan hai — aur unki us galti se hume genetics ka sabse zaroori sabak mila.

📌 Lakshan do tarah ke hote hain:
🔹 Anuvanshik (inherited) — jo gene se aate hain, maa-baap se mile hue
🔹 Arjit (acquired) — jo zindagi mein kamaye jaate hain, gene mein nahi hote

⚖️ Dono Mein Farak

Sirf wahi lakshan aage jaata hai jiska badlav germ cell (sperm/ovum) ke DNA tak pahunche.

📌 Ek line ka niyam:
Aapke shareer ki body cells (muscle, khaal, dimaag) mein hua koi bhi badlav bachchon tak nahi jaata — kyunki bachche sirf germ cells (sperm/ovum) se bante hain. Jab tak badlav germ cell ke DNA mein na ho, wo agli peedhi tak pahunch hi nahi sakta.

🦒 Lamarck Ki Mashhoor Galti

Jean-Baptiste Lamarck (1744–1829) ek bade french vaigyanik the. Unhone sabse pehle ye kaha ki jeev samay ke saath badalte hain — ye soch us zamaane mein bahut bahaduri wali thi. Par unka tareeka galat tha.

🦒 Zirafe Ki Gardan

Lamarck ka jawab: "Bahut saal pehle zirafon ki gardan chhoti thi. Unhe unche pedon ke patte khaane ke liye gardan kheenchni padi. Kheench-kheench kar gardan thodi lambi ho gayi. Phir unhone wo lambi gardan apne bachchon ko de di. Aisa peedhi-dar-peedhi hua aur gardan lambi hoti chali gayi."

Ise "Inheritance of Acquired Characters" kehte hain.

Galti kahan hai? Kheenchne se gardan ki body cells par asar pada, par zirafe ke germ cells (sperm/ovum) ke DNA mein koi badlav nahi hua. Toh wo "kamayi hui" lambai bachche tak pahunch hi nahi sakti thi.

Sahi jawab (Darwin ka): zirafon ki aabaadi mein pehle se hi thodi variation thi — kuch ki gardan zara lambi, kuch ki zara chhoti. Jinki gardan lambi thi wo unche patte kha paaye, behtar zinda rahe aur zyada bachche kar paaye. Peedhi-dar-peedhi lambi gardan wale gene badhte gaye. Gardan kisi ne "kamayi" nahi — jinke paas pehle se thi, wo bach gaye.

🔬 Weismann ka chuha prayog

1880 ke dashak mein August Weismann ne Lamarck ki soch ko seedha test kiya. Unhone chuhon ki poonch kaat di aur unse bachche paida karwaye. Phir un bachchon ki poonch kaat kar unse bhi bachche karwaye. Aisa unhone 21 peedhiyon tak kiya — 1592 chuhon par.

Nateeja? 22vi peedhi ka chuha bhi poori lambi poonch ke saath hi paida hua. Ek bhi bachcha chhoti poonch ke saath nahi aaya.

Yahi seedha sabak hai: shareer par kiya gaya badlav DNA nahi badalta. Isiliye peedhi-dar-peedhi kaan chhedne se koi bachcha chhide hue kaan ke saath paida nahi hota.

🧪 Khud Test Karo — 3 Sawaal

🔍 Teen sawaal ka test

1. Kya ye paida hote waqt tay ho gaya tha, ya baad mein aaya?
2. Kya iske liye koi gene zimmedaar hai?
3. Kya iska badlav germ cell ke DNA tak pahuncha?

⚠️ Ek nazuk baat — kad (height): Kad ka gene toh anuvanshik hai, par asli kad khaana bhi tay karta hai. Agar bachpan mein poshan na mile toh koi bachcha apni poori genetic lambai tak nahi pahunchta.

Isliye kai lakshan "gene + maahol" dono ka nateeja hote hain — sirf ek nahi. Isko "nature aur nurture" kaha jaata hai.

🌱 Toh Naye Lakshan Aate Kahan Se Hain?

Agar arjit lakshan aage nahi jaate, toh jeevon mein naye lakshan aaye kaise? Jawab hai: mutation.

📌 Mutation = DNA mein hui ek achanak galti (jaise copy karte waqt ek akshar badal jaana).

🔹 Agar mutation body cell mein ho → sirf usi vyakti par asar, aage nahi jaata
🔹 Agar mutation germ cell mein ho → bachchon tak jaata hai

Zyadatar mutation ka koi asar nahi hota, kuch nuksaandeh hote hain, aur kabhi-kabhi ek faayde ka nikal aata hai — aur wahi evolution ka kachcha maal ban jaata hai.

🩺 Sickle cell — ek hi galti, do nateeje. Sickle cell anaemia ek hi base ki galti se hota hai. Jinke paas dono kharab allele hon, unhe gambhir bimari hoti hai. Par jinke paas sirf ek ho (carrier), unhe bimari toh nahi hoti — aur unhe malaria se kuch bachaav bhi mil jaata hai!

Isiliye bharat ke un ilaakon mein jahan malaria zyada raha hai (jaise madhya bharat ke kai aadivasi ilaake), wahan sickle cell allele zyada paaya jaata hai. Bharat sarkaar ne 2023 mein "National Sickle Cell Anaemia Elimination Mission" shuru kiya hai, jiska lakshya un ilaakon mein ghar-ghar jaanch karna hai. Jaanch karwana samajhdari hai — isse pata chal jaata hai ki aap carrier hain ya nahi.

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat mein aksar kaha jaata hai ki "padhe-likhe maa-baap ke bachche tez hote hain". Ismein gene ka hissa bahut kam hai — asli wajah hai ki aise gharon mein bachchon ko kitaabein, achhi padhai aur samay milta hai.

Yahi is lesson ka sabse sundar sandesh hai: padhai anuvanshik nahi hai. Koi bhi bachcha, kisi bhi gaon ya parivaar se, agar use mauka aur achhi shiksha mile, toh sabse aage ja sakta hai. Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam ek nav (boat) banane wale ke bete the Rameswaram ke ek chhote se ghar se — aur desh ke rashtrapati aur mahaan vaigyanik bane.

Gene aapka rang aur kad tay karte hain. Aapki mehnat aur mauka aapka bhavishya tay karte hain.
