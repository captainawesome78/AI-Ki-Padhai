Sabse Pehla Sawaal 🤔

Aapki naak kis par gayi hai? Shayad papa par. Aankhein? Shayad mummy par. Aur haste waqt gaal par gadda (dimple)? Wo shayad daadi par gaya ho. Har parivaar mein ye baat hoti hai — par asal mein hota kya hai? Koi trait (lakshan) ek peedhi se doosri peedhi tak kaise pahunchta hai?

1860 ke aas-paas tak duniya ka jawab tha: "khoon mil jaata hai". Log maante the ki maa-baap ke trait rang ki tarah mil-jul jaate hain. Laal aur safed rang milaao toh gulaabi milta hai, na? Toh lamba + chhota = madhyam hona chahiye.

📌 Is soch ka naam tha "Blending Inheritance" (milne wali viraasat). Aur ye soch bilkul galat thi — par ise galat sabit karne ke liye kisi ne 8 saal tak matar ke paudhe uga-uga kar ginti ki. Unka naam tha Gregor Johann Mendel.

🤨 Blending galat kyun hai — ek seedha sawaal

Agar trait sach mein mil jaate, toh kuch peedhiyon baad duniya ke saare log ek jaise ho jaate — jaise sabhi rang milaakar ek slaty rang ban jaata hai. Par aisa hota nahi! Neeli aankhon wale bachche aaj bhi paida hote hain, chaahe unke maa-baap ki aankhein bhoori hon. Matlab trait ghulte nahi — wo chhupte hain aur phir wapas aate hain.

👨‍🔬 Mendel Kaun The?

Gregor Mendel (1822–1884) ek paadri (monk) the, jo aaj ke Czech Republic mein Brno naam ke shehar ke ek monastery mein rehte the. Wo garib kisan parivaar se the, aur unhone physics aur maths padhi thi. Unhone teacher banne ka exam do baar diya — aur dono baar fail ho gaye.

Par usi "fail" aadmi ne wo kar dikhaya jo koi bade professor nahi kar paaye — kyunki unke paas ek aisi cheez thi jo tab ke jeev-vigyaanikon ke paas nahi thi: maths ki soch. Unhone traits ko sirf dekha nahi, unhe gina.

🌿 Bageeche Mein 8 Saal

Monastery ke ek chhote se bageeche mein Mendel ne 1856 se 1863 tak matar (pea) ke paudhon par kaam kiya. Unhone 28,000 se zyada paudhe ugaye aur har ek ka trait likha — kitne lambe, kitne chhote, kitne peele beej, kitne hare.

1865 mein unhone apna kaam ek chhoti si local society ke saamne padha. Log baithe rahe, kuch samajh nahi aaya, taaliyan bajayin aur ghar chale gaye. Kisi ne dhyaan nahi diya.

Mendel 1884 mein guzar gaye — ye maankar ki unka kaam bekaar gaya. Phir 1900 mein, unki maut ke 16 saal baad, teen alag-alag vaigyanikon ne apne-apne prayog kiye aur paaya ki unke natije wahi hain — jo Mendel ne 35 saal pehle likh diye the. Tab jaakar duniya ne unhe "Father of Genetics" kaha.

🫛 Matar Hi Kyun? — Chunav Mein Hi Aadha Kaam Tha

Mendel ki sabse badi hoshiyari prayog mein nahi, paudha chunne mein thi. Matar (Pisum sativum) mein pancho baatein ekdum sahi thin:

Mendel ne saat aise trait chune jinke sirf do saaf-saaf roop the — koi "beech ka" roop nahi.

📌 Matar chunne ke 5 kaaran:
1️⃣ Har trait ke sirf do saaf roop — lamba ya chhota, koi "madhyam" nahi
2️⃣ Paudha jaldi badhta hai — ek saal mein kai peedhiyan
3️⃣ Ek hi phool mein nar aur maada dono ang — matlab khud se hi pollination (self-pollination) ho jaata hai
4️⃣ Chaaho toh haath se cross bhi kara sakte ho (anther kaat kar doosre ka pollen lagaao)
5️⃣ Ek paudhe se bahut saare beej — matlab ginti ke liye bada data

💡 Self-pollination (paraagan) ka bada faayda

Kyunki matar khud se pollinated ho jaata hai, Mendel ko "pure line" (shuddh vansh) paudhe aasaani se mil gaye — aise paudhe jinke bachche peedhi-dar-peedhi bilkul unhi jaise hote the. Lambe se hamesha lamba, chhote se hamesha chhota. Bina pure line ke poora prayog bekaar ho jaata — kyunki phir pata hi nahi chalta ki natija kis wajah se aaya.

🔬 Prayog Ka Tareeka — Step By Step

Mendel ka tareeka itna saaf tha ki aaj bhi science ke prayogon ka model maana jaata hai:

📋 Mendel ke 4 steps

Step 1 — Pure line taiyaar karo. Kai peedhiyon tak self-pollination karwaakar pakka karo ki lambe paudhe se sirf lambe, aur chhote se sirf chhote hi paida ho rahe hain.

Step 2 — Cross karo (P generation). Ek shuddh lambe paudhe ka pollen ek shuddh chhote paudhe ke stigma par lagaao. Lambe wale ke anther pehle hi kaat do taaki wo khud se pollinated na ho jaaye.

Step 3 — Bachchon ko dekho (F1 generation). Jo beej bane, unhe bo do aur dekho ki naye paudhe kaise dikhte hain.

Step 4 — F1 ko aapas mein cross karo (F2 generation). Aur ab sabse zaroori kaam — har ek paudhe ko gino.

Mendel ke asli aankde — F₂ mein 787 lambe aur 277 chhote paudhe mile, yani lagbhag 3:1.

🎯 Do Bade Nateeje

📌 Nateeja 1 — F1 mein sirf ek hi trait dikha. Chhota trait gaayab nahi hua tha, sirf chhup gaya tha. Jo trait dikhta hai use dominant kehte hain, aur jo chhup jaata hai use recessive.

📌 Nateeja 2 — F2 mein chhota trait wapas aa gaya, wo bhi 3:1 ke ratio (anupaat) mein. Iska matlab: trait ke "nirdesh" alag-alag kanon (particles) ki tarah rehte hain, jo aapas mein milte nahi. Mendel ne inhe "factors" kaha — aaj hum inhe gene kehte hain.

🇮🇳 India Connection

Mendel ke isi siddhaant par aaj bharat ki Harit Kranti (Green Revolution) tiki hai. 1960 ke dashak mein Dr. M. S. Swaminathan aur unki team ne gehun ki bauni (dwarf) kism ko zyada anaaj dene wali kism ke saath cross karaya. Bauna gehun kyun chahiye tha? Kyunki lamba gehun bhaari baali ke bojh se gir jaata tha aur fasal barbaad ho jaati thi.

Yahi soch chawal mein bhi lagi. Nateeja: 1960 mein bharat anaaj mangwaata tha, aaj duniya ka doosra sabse bada anaaj utpaadak hai. Ek monastery ke bageeche mein ginti gaye matar ke paudhon se lekar crore logon ki thaali tak — ye hai science ka safar.

🧐 Mendel ke aankde "bahut zyada sahi" the?

1936 mein statistics ke mahaan vaigyanik R. A. Fisher ne kaha ki Mendel ke natije 3:1 ke itne kareeb hain ki ittefaaq se aisa hona mushkil hai. Kuch logon ne shak kiya. Par aaj zyadatar vaigyanik maante hain ki Mendel ne dhokha nahi diya — shayad wo paudhe ginte waqt kuch saaf-saaf maamlon ko hi ginte the, ya unke sahayak ne unki ummeed ke hisaab se ginti ki. Poora siddhaant hazaron baar dobara jaanch kar sahi paaya gaya hai.
