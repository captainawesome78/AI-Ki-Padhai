Roti Ka 9 Metre Lamba Safar 🍽️

Aapne roti ka ek tukda khaya. Ab wo lagbhag 9 metre lambi nali (alimentary canal) se hokar guzrega, aur is safar mein use itna chhota kar diya jaayega ki wo khoon mein ghul sake. Isi ko pachan (digestion) kehte hain.

📌 Digestion: Bade, na-ghulne wale khaane ke anuon ko todkar chhote, ghulnsheel anuon mein badalna — taaki wo khoon mein soakhe (absorb) ja sakein.

1️⃣ Muh (Mouth) — Safar Ki Shuruaat

🦷 Daant khaane ko chabakar chhota karte hain (bhautik pachan)
💧 Laar (saliva) mein enzyme salivary amylase (ptyalin) hota hai jo starch → maltose (sugar) todta hai
👅 Jeebh khaane ko laar se milaati hai aur nigalne mein madad karti hai

🍚 Roti Ko Der Tak Chabao — Meethi Lagegi!

Ek prayog khud karo: roti ka tukda muh mein lo aur bina nigle 1-2 minute chabate raho. Kya hua? Wo halki meethi lagne lagegi! Kyun? Kyunki laar ke amylase ne roti ke starch ko todkar sugar (maltose) bana diya. Aapne apne muh mein hi ek chemical reaction hote hue chakh liya!

2️⃣ Grasnali (Oesophagus) — Peristalsis

Khaana muh se pet tak apne aap nahi girta — nali ki maanspeshiyan (muscles) laharon mein sikudti-failti hain aur khaane ko dhakelti hain. Isse peristalsis kehte hain.

🙃 Ulta Latak Kar Bhi Khaana Ja Sakta Hai!

Peristalsis ki wajah se agar aap seershasan (headstand) karte hue paani piyo, tab bhi wo pet tak pahunch jaayega — gravity ke khilaf! Kyunki maanspeshiyan use dhakel rahi hain, wo gir nahi raha. (Waise ye try mat karna 😄)

3️⃣ Aamashay (Stomach) — Acid Ki Duniya

Pet ki deewar se gastric juice nikalta hai, jisme teen cheezein hoti hain:

🧪 HCl (hydrochloric acid) — 2 kaam: (a) khaane ke saath aaye bacteria maarta hai, (b) medium ko amliya (acidic) banata hai taaki pepsin kaam kar sake
✂️ Pepsin — enzyme jo protein todta hai
🛡️ Mucus — pet ki apni deewar ko acid se bachata hai

🩺 Agar mucus ki parat kam ho jaaye toh acid pet ki apni deewar ko nuksaan pahunchata hai — isse ulcer (chhala) ban jaata hai. Kai baar iski wajah H. pylori naam ka bacteria hota hai, jiska ilaaj antibiotics se hota hai. Zyada tension, masaledaar khana aur samay par na khana bhi acidity badhate hain.

4️⃣ Chhoti Aant (Small Intestine) — Asli Kaam Yahan

Ye pachan tantra ka sabse lamba hissa hai (~6 metre) aur yahin pachan poora hota hai aur soakhna (absorption) bhi.

📌 Pachan ka final nateeja:
Carbohydrate → Glucose | Protein → Amino acids | Fat → Fatty acids + Glycerol

🧺 Villi — Tennis Court Jitna Area!

Chhoti aant ki andaruni deewar par laakhon ungli jaise ubhaar hote hain jinhe villi kehte hain. Inki wajah se soakhne ka area itna badh jaata hai ki agar poori aant ko khol kar failaya jaaye toh wo lagbhag ek tennis court jitni jagah gher legi! Har villus mein khoon ki naliyan hoti hain jo poshak tatvon ko seedhe khoon mein le leti hain.

5️⃣ Badi Aant (Large Intestine) Aur Ant

Yahan bacha hua paani soakh liya jaata hai, aur bina pacha kachra thos bankar mal dwar (anus) se bahar nikal jaata hai.

🥗 Resha (fibre) kyun zaroori hai? Sabzi, phal aur saabut anaaj ka fibre khud nahi pachta, par wo aant mein khaane ko aage badhne mein madad karta hai. Fibre kam khaane se kabz (constipation) ho jaati hai. Isiliye roz sabzi, phal aur khoob paani zaroori hai.

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat mein khaane ke saath hing, jeera, ajwain aur saunf daalne ki parampara hai — aur aaj vigyan maanta hai ki ye sach mein pachan mein madad karte hain aur gas kam karte hain. Khaane ke baad saunf khana sirf swaad ke liye nahi — wo digestive enzymes ko uttejit karti hai. Dadi-nani ka gyaan, ab lab mein bhi saabit.

Q2. Pet mein HCl ka kaam kya hai?
Q3. Bile (pitt ras) charbi ke saath kya karta hai?
