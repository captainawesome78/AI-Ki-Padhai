Shareer Ka Courier Service 🚚

Khaana pach gaya, oxygen mil gayi — par unhe shareer ke har ek cell tak pahunchana bhi toh hai! Aur wahan se kachra bhi uthana hai. Ye kaam karta hai parivahan tantra (circulatory system): dil, khoon aur rakt vahiniyan.

🩸 Khoon (Blood) — Bahta Hua Ootak

HissaKaam

RBC (laal koshikaayein)Haemoglobin se oxygen le jaati hain
WBC (safed koshikaayein)Bimari se ladti hain (rakshak sipaahi)

🩺 Anaemia Bharat mein bahut aam hai, khaas kar ladkiyon mein — iski wajah hai lohe (iron) ki kami, jisse haemoglobin kam ban paata hai. Lakshan: thakan, kamzori, chakkar, peelapan. Bachav: hari pattedar sabzi (palak), gud, chana, khajoor aur anaar. Iron ke saath Vitamin C (nimbu) lene se iron behtar soakha jaata hai.

🫀 Dil (Heart) — 4 Kamre

Manushya ka dil ek muthi jitna hai, par din-raat bina ruke chalta hai. Isme chaar kamre (chambers) hain:

ChamberKya aata haiKahan se / Kahan jaata hai
Right atrium (daayan alind)Deoxygenated khoonPoore shareer se (vena cava)
Right ventricle (daayan nilay)Deoxygenated khoonPhephdon ko bhejta hai
Left atrium (baayan alind)Oxygenated khoonPhephdon se aata hai
Left ventricle (baayan nilay)Oxygenated khoonPoore shareer ko bhejta hai

💪 Left ventricle ki deewar sabse moti kyun hai? Kyunki use khoon poore shareer mein (sar se pair tak) pump karna hai — bahut zyada dabaav chahiye. Right ventricle ko sirf paas ke phephdon tak bhejna hai, isliye uski deewar patli hai. Ye board mein poocha jaata hai!

🚪 Valves (kapaat) ka kaam: khoon ko sirf ek disha mein jaane dena — wapas nahi aane dena. Dil ki "lub-dub" ki aawaz inhi valves ke band hone se aati hai!

🔁 Double Circulation

📌 Manushya mein khoon ek chakkar mein do baar dil se guzarta hai — isiliye ise double circulation kehte hain.

1️⃣ Pulmonary circulation (chhota chakkar):
Dil (right side) → Phephde → Dil (left side)
Yahan khoon oxygen leta hai

2️⃣ Systemic circulation (bada chakkar):
Dil (left side) → Poora shareer → Dil (right side)
Yahan khoon oxygen deta hai aur CO₂ leta hai

💡 Double circulation ka faayda: Oxygenated aur deoxygenated khoon bilkul nahi milte — isliye shareer ko hamesha poori oxygen wala khoon milta hai. Isi wajah se manushya aur pakshi garam-khoon wale (warm-blooded) ho paate hain aur itni energy bana paate hain.

🚰 Artery vs Vein

Dhamani (Artery)Shira (Vein)
DishaDil se door le jaati haiDil ki taraf laati hai
DeewarMoti aur lachili (zyada dabaav)Patli
ValvesNahiHaan (khoon wapas na ho)
KhoonOxygenated (chhod kar pulmonary artery)Deoxygenated (chhod kar pulmonary vein)

⚠️ Do apwaad yaad rakho:
Pulmonary artery — artery hai par deoxygenated khoon le jaati hai (dil se phephdon tak)
Pulmonary vein — vein hai par oxygenated khoon laati hai (phephdon se dil tak)
Board mein ye zaroor poocha jaata hai!

🕸️ Capillaries (kaeshikaayein): Sabse patli naliyan — itni patli ki unki deewar sirf ek cell moti hoti hai! Isi wajah se yahin par khoon aur cells ke beech oxygen, khana aur kachre ka aadan-pradaan hota hai.

🌲 Paani 100 Metre Upar Kaise Chadhta Hai?

Ek bade ped mein paani jadon se pattiyon tak — kabhi 100 metre se bhi zyada upar — bina kisi pump ke kaise pahunchta hai? Jawaab hai transpiration pull: pattiyon se paani vaashp bankar udta hai, jisse ek kheenchav (suction) banta hai jo neeche se paani ki poori dori ko upar kheench leta hai. Prakriti ka apna pump — bina bijli ke!

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat mein har saal lakhon log rakt daan (blood donation) karte hain. Ek yunit khoon teen zindagiyan bacha sakta hai, kyunki use RBC, plasma aur platelets mein alag kiya ja sakta hai. 18 saal ke baad koi bhi swasth vyakti har 3 mahine mein rakt daan kar sakta hai — aur shareer wo khoon kuch hi hafton mein wapas bana leta hai.
