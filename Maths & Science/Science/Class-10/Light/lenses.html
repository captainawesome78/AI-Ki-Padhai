Mirror Se Lens Tak 🔄

Mirror (darpan) roshni ko wapas laut'ata hai — wo reflection (paraavartan) par chalta hai. Lens roshni ko paar guzarne deta hai — wo refraction (apvartan) par chalta hai.

Lens ek paardarshi kaanch ka tukda hai jiski do satah hoti hain (kam se kam ek curvature wali). Roshni pehle ghuste waqt mudti hai, phir nikalte waqt — aur dono mudav mil kar wo kamaal karte hain jise hum lens kehte hain.

📌 Do prakaar ke lens:
🔹 Convex (uttal) lens — beech mein mota, kinaron par patla. Roshni ko jodta (converge) hai. Isliye ise converging lens bhi kehte hain.
🔹 Concave (avatal) lens — beech mein patla, kinaron par mota. Roshni ko failata (diverge) hai. Isliye diverging lens.

💡 Ek chhota confusion door kar lo

Mirror mein concave jodta tha, par lens mein convex jodta hai! Ulta lagta hai na?

Wajah saaf hai: mirror mein roshni lautti hai, lens mein paar jaati hai. Concave mirror ki dhansi satah rays ko andar ki taraf mod deti hai; convex lens ka mota beech rays ko andar ki taraf mod deta hai. Dono jodte hain, par alag tareeke se.

Yaad rakho: "MO-JO" — MOta beech wala lens JOdta hai.

📍 Lens Ke Zaroori Bindu

Convex lens rays ko F₂ par jodta hai (real focus). Concave lens unhe failata hai — wo F₁ se aati hui lagti hain (virtual focus).

📌 Lens ke DO focus hote hain — F₁ aur F₂ — dono taraf! Kyunki roshni lens se dono taraf se guzar sakti hai. Mirror mein aisa nahi tha (wahan roshni sirf ek hi taraf se aati hai).

⚠️ Aur mirror ke ulte, lens mein R = 2f wala rishta laagu nahi hota. Lens ke liye hum sirf f aur 2f ki baat karte hain.

📐 Lens Ke 3 Ray Rules

Mirror mein 4 niyam the, lens mein sirf 3 — aur ye aur bhi aasaan hain.

Lens ke teen ray rules. Niyam 3 (optical centre se seedhi) sabse aasaan hai — ise hamesha use karo.

📌 Teen niyam:
1️⃣ Axis ke parallel aayi ray → refraction ke baad F₂ se guzarti hai (concave mein F₁ se aati hui lagti hai)
2️⃣ F₁ se hokar aayi ray → refraction ke baad axis ke parallel
3️⃣ Optical centre (O) se guzarti ray → bina mude seedhi nikal jaati hai

Tip: Niyam 1 + Niyam 3 sabse aasaan jodi hai — dono banane mein 5 second lagte hain.

🎯 Bilkul mirror wala pattern! Convex lens ka image hamesha real aur inverted hota hai — sivaay jab object F₁ aur O ke beech ho. Tab wo virtual, erect aur bada deta hai.

Aur wahi ek case hi magnifying glass (aatish shisha) hai! Isiliye padhne wale lens ko cheez ke bahut paas rakha jaata hai.

🔍 Magnifying glass ka raaz

Jab aap magnifying glass se kuch padhte hain, toh aap use akshar ke bahut paas rakhte hain — F se bhi paas.

Ab dhyaan se dekho: agar aap lens ko dheere-dheere door le jaayein, ek jagah aayegi jahan akshar dhundhle ho jaayenge (wo F hai), aur usse aage jaate hi achhanak akshar ULTE dikhne lagenge!

Kyun? Kyunki ab object F se pare chala gaya — aur table ke hisaab se image real aur inverted ho gaya. Ye ek chhota sa prayog hai jo aap abhi ghar par kar sakte hain.

🔵 Concave Lens — Hamesha Ek Jaisa

📌 Concave lens ka image hamesha:
🔹 Virtual
🔹 Erect
🔹 Diminished (chhota)
🔹 Aur hamesha O aur F₁ ke beech, object ki hi taraf

Bilkul convex mirror ki tarah — object kahin bhi rakho, jawab nahi badalta.

Kahan use hota haiKaunsa lensKyun

Door drishti dosh (myopia) ka chashmaConcaveRays ko thoda faila kar image retina par le aata hai
Nikat drishti dosh (hypermetropia) ka chashmaConvexRays ko jodkar image retina par laata hai

👓 Chashme ka number kaisa lens hai? Agar aapke chashme ka number minus (jaise −2.5) hai, toh aapke lens concave hain — aapko myopia (door ki cheezein dhundhli) hai. Agar number plus (jaise +1.5) hai, toh lens convex hain — hypermetropia (paas ki cheezein dhundhli).

Ye "number" asal mein lens ka power hai, jo agle lesson mein aa raha hai. Aur haan — aankhein har 1-2 saal mein check karwana zaroori hai, khaaskar agar aap zyada der phone ya kitaab paas se dekhte hain. Har din kuch der bahar khuli dhoop mein khelna bachchon mein myopia ka khatra kam karta hai — ye kai research mein saabit hua hai.

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat mein karodon logon ki nazar sirf isliye kharab hai kyunki unhe sahi chashma nahi mila — bimari nahi, bas ek lens ki kami. Ise "uncorrected refractive error" kehte hain, aur ye duniya mein drishti kamzori ka sabse bada kaaran hai.

Isi wajah se "National Programme for Control of Blindness" ke tehat sarkaari schoolon mein muft aankh jaanch aur muft chashme baante jaate hain. Ek study mein dekha gaya ki chashma milne ke baad bachchon ke exam ke number kaafi behtar ho gaye — kyunki ab unhe blackboard dikhne laga.

Sochiye: ek 200 rupaye ka convex ya concave lens, aur ek bachche ki poori padhai bach gayi. Yahi physics ki sabse seedhi taakat hai.
