Spherical Mirror — Concave Aur Convex 🥄

Ek Chammach Se Shuruaat 🥄

Ghar se ek chamakta hua chammach uthao aur dono taraf se apna chehra dekho.

🔹 Andar wali (gehri) taraf se dekho — paas se dekhne par chehra bada aur seedha dikhega. Ab chammach ko door le jao — achanak chehra ULTA ho jaayega! Ye concave (avatal) mirror (darpan) hai.
🔹 Bahar wali (ubhri) taraf se dekho — chehra hamesha chhota aur seedha dikhega, chaahe paas rakho ya door. Ye convex (uttal) mirror hai.

📌 Spherical mirror (gola darpan) ek gend (sphere) ke kate hue tukde jaisa hota hai.
🔹 Concave — reflection (paraavartan) andar wali (dhansi hui) satah se. Ye roshni ko ek jagah jodta (converge) hai.
🔹 Convex — reflection bahar wali (ubhri) satah se. Ye roshni ko faila (diverge) deta hai.

💡 Naam yaad rakhne ka aasaan tareeka

CONCAVE mein "CAVE" (gufa) chhupa hai — gufa andar dhansi hoti hai. Toh concave = andar dhansa hua.

Aur concave = Converging (jodne wala), convex = Diverging (failane wala). Dono "C" saath yaad karo.

📍 Chaar Zaroori Bindu Aur Rekhaein

Aage badhne se pehle ye chaar shabd pakke kar lo — poora chapter inhi par chalega.

Concave mirror ke chaar zaroori bindu. Parallel rays reflection ke baad F par milti hain — isliye ise "converging" mirror kehte hain.

📌 Sabse zaroori rishta — yaad kar lo:

R = 2f   yani   f = R/2

Focus hamesha P aur C ke bilkul beech mein hota hai. Numericals mein aksar R diya hota hai aur f chahiye hota hai — ya ulta. Ye ek line bahut number bachaayegi.

🤔 Convex mirror ka focus kahan hai?

Convex mirror parallel rays ko faila deta hai — wo kabhi milti nahi! Toh focus hai kahan?

Un failti hui rays ko peeche ki taraf badhaao — wo mirror ke PEECHE ek bindu par milti hui lagti hain. Wahi convex mirror ka focus hai.

Isiliye convex mirror ka focus virtual hai aur uska f positive hota hai (sign convention mein). Concave ka focus saamne hai, isliye uska f negative.

📐 Ray Diagram Ke 4 Niyam

Image kahan banega — ye pata karne ke liye hume sirf do rays khinchni hoti hain. Par chunne ke liye chaar aasaan niyam hain. Koi bhi do chun lo.

Chaar ray (kiran) rules. Niyam 1 aur 2 ek doosre ke ULTE hain — aur zyadatar sawaalon mein yahi do sabse aasaan padte hain.

📌 Practical tip: zyadatar sawaalon mein Niyam 1 + Niyam 3 sabse aasaan hai. Ek ray object ke sire se parallel bhejo (wo F se guzregi), doosri C se bhejo (wo wapas aayegi). Jahan dono milein — wahin image ka sira hai.

Convex mirror mein bhi wahi chaar niyam lagte hain — bas F aur C mirror ke peeche hote hain, isliye rays unki taraf badhti hui maani jaati hain.

🎯 Poori table ek line mein: concave mirror ka image hamesha real aur inverted hota hai — sivaay ek haalat ke: jab object F aur P ke beech ho. Sirf wahi ek case virtual, erect aur bada deta hai.

Aur yahi wo case hai jo aapne chammach ke paas dekha tha!

💡 Ek aur pattern jo yaad rakhna aasaan hai

Dhyaan do: object aur image aapas mein jagah badal sakte hain!

Object C se pare → image F aur C ke beech.
Object F aur C ke beech → image C se pare.

Yani agar aap object ko image ki jagah rakh dein, toh image object ki jagah ban jaayega. Ise principle of reversibility kehte hain — roshni ka raasta hamesha ulta bhi utna hi sahi hota hai.

🔵 Convex Mirror — Hamesha Ek Jaisa

Convex mirror ki kahani bahut chhoti hai — kyunki uska jawab hamesha ek hi hai.

📌 Convex mirror ka image hamesha:
🔹 Virtual (mirror ke peeche)
🔹 Erect (seedha)
🔹 Diminished (chhota)

Object kahin bhi rakho — paas, door, bahut door — jawab kabhi nahi badalta. Bas image thoda aur chhota hota jaata hai.

🚗 Isiliye Gaadi Mein Convex Mirror Lagta Hai

Gaadi ke side mirror convex hote hain. Kyun?

✅ Faayda: chhota image banane ka matlab hai ki bahut zyada ilaaka ek hi chhote mirror mein aa jaata hai — yani wider field of view. Driver ko peeche ka bahut bada hissa dikh jaata hai.

✅ Image hamesha seedha rehta hai — ulta hota toh driver confuse ho jaata.

⚠️ Nuksaan: cheezein choti dikhti hain, isliye door lagti hain. Isi wajah se un mirror par likha hota hai: "Objects in mirror are closer than they appear".
