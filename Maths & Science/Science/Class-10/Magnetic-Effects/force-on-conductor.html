Ulta Sawaal 🔄

Ab tak humne dekha: current se magnetic field banta hai. Ab ek ulta sawaal poochhte hain:

Agar ek current wale taar ko kisi doosre magnet ke field mein rakh dein, toh kya hoga?

📌 Taar ka apna field magnet ke field se takraata hai — aur nateeja ye hota hai ki taar par ek FORCE (dhakka) lagta hai. Taar hilne lagta hai!

Aur yahi ek baat poori duniya ki har motor ka aadhaar hai — pankha, mixer, washing machine, lift, train, hard disk. Sab isi force par ghoomte hain.

🧪 Classic Prayog

Ek aluminium ki patli chhad (rod) ko do taaron se latka do, aur use ek horseshoe magnet ke do poles ke beech rakh do.

Ab battery se current chalu karo — chhad achanak ek taraf jhatka khaakar hil jaayegi!

Ab do cheezein aazmao:
🔹 Current ULTA karo → chhad doosri taraf hilegi
🔹 Magnet ULTA karo (N-S badal do) → chhad phir doosri taraf hilegi
🔹 Dono ulta karo → chhad wapas pehli taraf hilegi!

✋ Fleming Ka Left-Hand Rule

Force kis taraf lagega — ye batane ke liye ek bahut mashhoor tareeka hai.

📌 Fleming ka LEFT-hand rule:

Apne ULTE (left) haath ki teen ungliyan ek doosre ke perpendicular (90°) failaao — angootha, pehli ungli aur beech wali ungli.

🖕 Pehli ungli (forefinger) = FIELD (B), N se S ki taraf
🖕 Beech wali ungli (middle) = CURRENT (I)
👍 Angootha (thumb) = FORCE (F) — yahi jawab hai

⚠️ ULTA haath hi — seedha haath lagaya toh jawab bilkul ulta aayega!

Fleming ka left-hand rule. Yaad rakhne ka tareeka: angootha se shuru karo — Force, phir First finger = Field, phir seCond finger = Current.
🧮 Hal Kiya Hua Sawaal

Ek taar mein current daayen (east) ki taraf bah raha hai. Magnetic field page ke andar hai. Force kis taraf lagega?

Hal — ULTA haath uthao:
🔹 Pehli ungli ko page ke andar karo (field)
🔹 Beech wali ungli ko daayen karo (current)
🔹 Ab angootha apne aap UPAR ki taraf ho jaayega

✅ Jawab: force UPAR ki taraf lagega.

Jaanch: ab current ulta karo (baayen) — angootha neeche ho jaayega. Sahi!

🎯 Force kab sabse zyada aur kab ZERO hota hai:

✅ Force sabse zyada tab hota hai jab current field ke bilkul perpendicular (90°) ho
❌ Force bilkul ZERO ho jaata hai jab current field ke saath-saath (parallel) ho

Ye exam mein bahut poocha jaata hai. Wajah aasaan hai: agar taar field ke saath hi hai, toh "kaatne" ke liye kuch bacha hi nahi — dono ek hi disha mein hain.

💪 Force kitna bada hoga — teen baatein:
1️⃣ Current (I) zyada → force zyada
2️⃣ Field (B) taakatwar → force zyada
3️⃣ Field mein taar ki lambai (L) zyada → force zyada

🤔 Ek gehri baat — force asal mein kis par lagta hai?

Force "taar" par nahi, taar ke andar chalte hue electrons par lagta hai!

Har chalta hua charge magnetic field mein ek force mehsoos karta hai. Wo electrons taar ki deewar se takra kar wo force poore taar tak pahuncha dete hain — aur taar hil jaata hai.

Isiliye agar current band kar do toh force bhi turant khatam — kyunki ab koi charge chal hi nahi raha. Aur isiliye ek ruka hua (stationary) charge magnetic field mein koi force mehsoos nahi karta.

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat ki Vande Bharat aur metro trains electric motor se chalti hain — aur har motor ke andar bilkul yahi force kaam kar raha hota hai jo aap abhi padh rahe ho.

Aur ek aur mazedaar baat: ye trains rukte waqt "regenerative braking" istemaal karti hain — yani rukte samay wahi motor ulta chal kar generator ban jaata hai, aur train ki gati ko wapas bijli mein badal kar grid ko lauta deta hai!

Delhi Metro ne isi tareeke se itni bijli bachai ki use United Nations se carbon credit mila — duniya ki pehli metro jise ye samman mila. Ek chapter ka ek rule, aur usse desh ki crore rupaye aur hazaron ton CO₂ ki bachat.
