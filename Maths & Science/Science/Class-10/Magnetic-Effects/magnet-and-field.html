Bina Chhue Kaam Karne Wali Cheez 🪄

Ek magnet ko lohe ki keel ke paas le jao — keel khinch kar chali aayegi, bina magnet ke chhue. Do magnet ke ek jaise sire paas laao — wo haath se hi dhakka mehsoos karaate hain.

Toh beech ki khaali jagah mein aisa kya hai? Wahan kuch dikhta toh nahi. Par kuch hai zaroor — aur usi ko hum magnetic field kehte hain.

📌 Magnetic field = magnet ke aas-paas ka wo ilaaka jahan uska asar mehsoos hota hai.

Ye field dikhti nahi, par uska asar dikhta hai — aur usi asar se hum uska naksha bana lete hain. Us naksha ki rekhaon ko magnetic field lines kehte hain.

🧭 Prithvi khud ek bahut bada magnet hai

Compass ki sui hamesha uttar ki taraf kyun ghoomti hai? Kyunki prithvi khud ek magnet hai — uske andar pighle hue lohe ke behne se ek bahut bada magnetic field banta hai.

Aur ek mazedaar ulti baat: compass ka jo sira uttar ki taraf jaata hai use "north pole (dhruv)" kehte hain. Par magnet mein ulte sire hi khinchte hain! Iska matlab hai ki prithvi ke bhaugolik uttar (geographic north) ke paas asal mein prithvi ka magnetic SOUTH pole hai.

Isi field ki wajah se sooraj se aane wale khatarnaak kan prithvi tak nahi pahunch paate — yani prithvi ka magnetic field ek dhaal (shield) ki tarah hume bachata hai. Aur wahi kan jab dhruvon ke paas takraate hain toh aasman mein rangeen roshni banti hai, jise Aurora kehte hain.

🔍 Field Lines Ko Dekho Kaise

Field lines koi kalpana nahi hain — aap unhe ghar par dekh sakte ho.

🧪 Khud Karke Dekho — Lohe ka Chura

Chahiye: ek bar magnet, ek kaagaz ya kaanch ki plate, aur thoda lohe ka chura (iron filings). Chura nahi hai? Lohe ki cheez ko reti (file) se ghis kar bana sakte ho.

Tareeka:
1️⃣ Magnet ko table par rakho aur uspar kaagaz rakh do
2️⃣ Kaagaz par lohe ka chura halke se chhidko
3️⃣ Ab kaagaz ko ungli se halke se thapthapaao

Kya hoga: chura apne aap saaf-saaf rekhaon mein sajj jaayega! Har chhota kan khud ek chhota magnet ban kar field ki disha mein mud jaata hai.

😲 Aap dekh paaoge ki rekhaein dono poles ke paas sabse ghani hain — kyunki wahin field sabse taakatwar hai.

Bar magnet ki field lines. Bahar N se S ki taraf, aur magnet ke ANDAR S se N — isliye har line ek poora band chakra banati hai.

📜 Field Lines Ke 5 Niyam

📌 Ye paanch niyam exam mein seedhe poochhe jaate hain:

1️⃣ Magnet ke BAHAR lines N se S ki taraf chalti hain

2️⃣ Magnet ke ANDAR lines S se N ki taraf chalti hain — isliye har line ek band chakra (closed loop) hoti hai

3️⃣ Lines kabhi ek doosre ko KAATTI nahi hain

4️⃣ Lines jahan ghani (paas-paas) hon wahan field taakatwar, aur jahan door-door hon wahan kamzor

5️⃣ Kisi bindu par field ki disha = wahan line par khinchi gayi tangent (sparsh rekha) ki disha

❓ Do field lines kabhi kaat kyun nahi sakti?

Ye exam mein bahut poocha jaata hai, aur jawab bahut sundar hai.

Maan lo do lines kisi bindu par kaat gayin. Us bindu par do tangent banengi — yani field ki do alag dishaayein.

Par ek hi jagah par rakhi compass ki sui do taraf ek saath kaise ghoomegi? Wo namumkin hai. Sui hamesha ek hi disha dikhaayegi.

Isliye do field lines kabhi kaat nahi sakti. Ek bindu par field ki sirf ek disha ho sakti hai.

🎯 Magnetic field ek "vector" raashi hai — yani usme maap (magnitude) aur disha dono hoti hain. Isiliye field lines par hamesha teer (arrow) lagaya jaata hai.

Sirf "field kitna hai" kehna adhoora hai — "kis taraf hai" batana bhi zaroori hai.

✂️ Magnet ko todne par kya hota hai?

Ek bar magnet ko beech se tod do. Kya ek tukde mein sirf N aur doosre mein sirf S bachega?

Nahi! Dono tukdon mein phir se N aur S dono aa jaate hain. Aur unhe phir todo — phir wahi. Chaahe kitna bhi chhota tukda kar lo.

Wajah: har magnet asal mein karodon bahut chhote magnet ka jamaawada hai, jo sab ek hi disha mein sajje hue hain. Todne par bas chhote-chhote jamaawade ban jaate hain — par har ek ke apne do sire rehte hain.

Isiliye akela magnetic pole (monopole) aaj tak kabhi nahi mila — aur ye physics ke sabse bade khule sawaalon mein se ek hai.

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat mein magnetic field ka adhyayan bahut purana hai. Alibag (Maharashtra) mein 1904 se ek magnetic observatory chal rahi hai, jo aaj bhi prithvi ke magnetic field ko lagataar naapti hai — duniya ki sabse purani lagataar chalne wali observatories mein se ek.

Ye observatory Mumbai se yahan isliye laayi gayi thi kyunki Mumbai mein bijli ki trams chalne lagi thin, aur unke current se banne wale magnetic field se naap kharab ho rahi thi — yani wahi cheez jo aap is chapter mein padh rahe ho!

Aaj Indian Institute of Geomagnetism is kaam ko aage badhata hai, aur uske aankde satellite, navigation aur bhukamp ke adhyayan mein kaam aate hain.
