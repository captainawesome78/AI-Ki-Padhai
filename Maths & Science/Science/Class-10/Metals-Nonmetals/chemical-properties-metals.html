Dhaatuein React Kaise Karti Hain? ⚗️

Dhaatuein electron dene mein maahir hoti hain — isiliye wo positive ion (cation) banati hain. Ab dekhte hain wo oxygen, paani aur acid ke saath kya karti hain.

1️⃣ Metal + Oxygen → Metal Oxide

📐 Metal + O₂ → Metal oxide (ye basic oxide hota hai)

✔ Examples

4Na + O₂ → 2Na₂O
2Mg + O₂ → 2MgO
4Al + 3O₂ → 2Al₂O₃
2Cu + O₂ → 2CuO (kaala)

🛡️ Aluminium Zang Kyun Nahi Pakadta?

Aluminium bahut reactive hai — phir bhi khidki-darwaze saalon tak theek rehte hain, kyun? Kyunki aluminium hawa se milte hi apni satah par Al₂O₃ ki ek bahut patli, majboot aur chipki hui parat bana leta hai. Ye parat andar ki dhaatu ko hawa se bacha leti hai! Isi ko jaan-boojh kar mota karne ki prakriya anodising kehlati hai.

🔸 Amphoteric oxides: Kuch metal oxides (jaise Al₂O₃ aur ZnO) acid aur base dono ke saath react karte hain! Ye board ka important point hai:
Al₂O₃ + 6HCl → 2AlCl₃ + 3H₂O  (base ki tarah)
Al₂O₃ + 2NaOH → 2NaAlO₂ + H₂O  (acid ki tarah)

2️⃣ Metal + Paani → Metal Hydroxide/Oxide + H₂

Sab dhaatuein paani se react nahi karti — ye unki reactivity par depend karta hai:

DhaatuThande paani seReaction
K, NaBahut tezi se, aag lag jaati hai2Na + 2H₂O → 2NaOH + H₂
CaDheere, bubbles ke saathCa + 2H₂O → Ca(OH)₂ + H₂
MgThande se nahi, garam paani seMg + 2H₂O → Mg(OH)₂ + H₂
Al, Zn, FeSirf bhaap (steam) se3Fe + 4H₂O → Fe₃O₄ + 4H₂
Cu, Ag, AuBilkul nahiKoi reaction nahi
💥 Calcium Ka Mazedaar Vyavhaar

Calcium ko paani mein daalo toh wo tairne lagta hai! Kyun? Kyunki reaction se bani hydrogen gas ke bubbles uski satah par chipak jaate hain aur use upar utha dete hain. Sodium ke saath toh nikli garmi itni hoti hai ki hydrogen turant aag pakad leti hai — isiliye sodium ko kabhi paani ke paas nahi rakhte.

3️⃣ Metal + Acid → Salt + H₂

📐 Metal + dilute acid → Salt + Hydrogen gas

✔ Examples

Zn + 2HCl → ZnCl₂ + H₂
Mg + 2HCl → MgCl₂ + H₂
Fe + H₂SO₄ → FeSO₄ + H₂

⚠️ Cu, Ag, Au acid se hydrogen nahi dete — kyunki ye hydrogen se kam reactive hain (reactivity series mein H ke neeche hain). Isiliye tambe ke bartan mein khatti cheezein rakhna surakshit hai... par lambe samay tak nahi!

⚠️ Nitric acid (HNO₃) apwaad hai — ye ek strong oxidising agent hai, isliye wo hydrogen ki jagah nitrogen ke oxides (NO, NO₂) deta hai. Sirf Mg aur Mn hi bahut dilute HNO₃ se H₂ dete hain.

🤝 Metal + Non-metal → Ionic Compound

Sabse important reaction — jab dhaatu apne electron de deti hai aur adhaatu unhe le leti hai. Isse ionic (electrovalent) compound banta hai.

✔ Example: Namak banna

2Na + Cl₂ → 2NaCl
Sodium apna 1 electron chlorine ko de deta hai → Na⁺ aur Cl⁻ ban jaate hain, jo ek doosre ko kheenchte hain.
