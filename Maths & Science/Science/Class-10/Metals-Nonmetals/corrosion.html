Metals Ki Dheemi Maut 🛡️

Chapter ka aakhri hissa. Metals hawa, nami aur gases ke sampark mein aakar dheere-dheere kharab hoti rehti hain — isse corrosion (ksharan) kehte hain. Ye ek oxidation (oxeekaran) reaction (abhikriya) hai.

Metal (dhaatu)Kya banta haiDikhta kaisa hai
Loha (Fe)Hydrated iron oxide (zang/rust)Bhoori-laal, bhurbhuri parat
Chandi (Ag)Silver sulphide (Ag₂S)Kaali parat
Tamba (Cu)Basic copper carbonateHari parat
Aluminium (Al)Al₂O₃Safed — par ye bachati hai!

⚠️ Zang aur Al₂O₃ mein bada farak: Zang bhurbhura hota hai aur jhad jaata hai — jisse andar ka naya loha phir hawa ke sampark mein aata hai aur zang badhta rehta hai, jab tak poora loha khatm na ho jaaye. Par aluminium ki oxide parat chipki hui aur majboot hoti hai — wo andar ki metal ko bacha leti hai. Isiliye loha sadta hai, aluminium nahi.

💧 Zang Lagne Ke Liye Kya Chahiye?

📌 Zang lagne ke liye DONO zaroori hain:
1️⃣ Oxygen (hawa)  aur  2️⃣ Paani / nami

In dono mein se koi ek bhi hata do, toh zang ruk jaata hai. Sara bachav isi ek baat par tika hai!

🌊 Samundar Ke Paas Zang Zyada Kyun?

Tateey (coastal) ilaakon mein gaadiyan aur pul zyada jaldi zang pakadte hain, kyunki hawa mein namak (salt) aur nami dono zyada hoti hai — aur namak wala paani bijli achhi conduct karta hai, jisse corrosion ki electrochemical reaction tez ho jaati hai. Isiliye Mumbai aur Chennai mein gaadiyon ko zyada dekhbhaal chahiye hoti hai!

🛡️ Zang Se Bachne Ke Tareeke

TareekaKaise kaam karta haiKahan use hota hai
PaintingHawa-paani se parat bana deta haiGrill, gate, pul, gaadi
Oiling / greasingChikni parat nami rokti haiMachine ke parts, auzaar
GalvanisationLohe par zinc ki parat chadhanaChhat ki chaadar, keel, pipe
ElectroplatingBijli se doosri metal ki parat chadhanaGehne, nal, bartan
AlloyingDusri metal milakar zang-rodhi bananaStainless steel

💡 Galvanisation itna khaas kyun hai? Zinc, lohe se zyada reactive hai — isliye wo pehle khud react karta hai aur lohe ko bachaata hai. Aur mazedaar baat: agar parat kahin se khiskh bhi jaaye, tab bhi zinc lohe ko bachata rehta hai! Isiliye ise "sacrificial protection" (balidaan wali suraksha) kehte hain.

Zang ke liye hawa AUR paani dono chahiye — isliye kisi ek ko rok dena hi bachaav hai.

⚗️ Alloys — Metals Ka Mishran

📌 Alloy (mishradhatu): Do ya zyada metals ka (ya metal + non-metal ka) samaangi mishran. Alloy banane se metal zyada majboot, zang-rodhi aur upyogi ho jaati hai.

AlloyKis se banaKahan use hota hai
Stainless steelFe + Cr + Ni (+C)Bartan, chhuri, aujaar — zang nahi lagta
Brass (pital)Cu + ZnBartan, murti, taale
Bronze (kaansa)Cu + SnMurtiyan, ghanti, medal
SolderPb + SnBijli ke taar jodna (kam M.P.)
AmalgamKoi metal + mercuryDaanton ki filling
💍 24 Carat vs 22 Carat Sona

Shuddh sona (24 carat) itna naram hota hai ki usse gehne ban hi nahi sakte — wo mud jaayenge! Isliye usme thoda tamba ya chandi milaya jaata hai, jisse 22 carat sona banta hai (22 bhaag sona + 2 bhaag doosri metal). Ye kada hota hai aur gehne banane layak. Toh agli baar jab koi "22 carat" kahe — aapko pata hai wo ek alloy ki baat kar raha hai!

📖 Chapter Summary

✅ Metals: chamak, malleable, ductile, conductor, sonorous | Non-metals: brittle, dull, insulator
✅ Apwaad: Hg (drav metal), Br (drav non-metal), graphite (conductor non-metal), Na-K (naram)
✅ Reactivity series: K > Na > Ca > Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > Pb > H > Cu > Hg > Ag > Au
✅ Ionic bond = electron ka poora transfer; lattice; pighle par bijli conduct
✅ Metallurgy: concentration → roasting/calcination → reduction (apchayan) → refining
✅ Corrosion ke liye oxygen + paani dono chahiye; bachav: paint, grease, galvanisation, alloy
