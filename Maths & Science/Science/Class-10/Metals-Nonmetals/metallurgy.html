Patthar Se Dhaatu Tak 🏭

Zyadatar dhaatuein prakriti mein shuddh nahi milti — wo compounds ke roop mein patthar ke andar chhupi hoti hain. Unhe nikaalne, saaf karne aur istemal layak banane ki poori prakriya ko metallurgy kehte hain.

📌 Minerals: Prithvi mein maujood wo prakritik padarth jinme dhaatu ke compounds hote hain.
📌 Ores (ayask): Wo minerals jinse dhaatu nikalna faydemand (economical) ho.
💡 Har ore ek mineral hai, par har mineral ore nahi hota.

🪜 Extraction Ke 4 Bade Steps

Step 1 — Concentration (Enrichment)

Ore ke saath mitti-patthar (gangue) mila hota hai. Use hataakar ore ko saaf karna. Iske tareeke: hand-picking, washing, froth flotation (sulphide ores ke liye), magnetic separation.

Metallurgy ke 4 steps — aur reduction ka tareeka dhaatu ki reactivity se tay hota hai.

Step 2 — Ore Ko Oxide Mein Badalna

Dhaatu nikaalna oxide se sabse aasaan hai, isliye pehle ore ko oxide banate hain:

🔥 Roasting — sulphide ore ko hawa (oxygen) ki maujoodgi mein tez garam karna:
2ZnS + 3O₂ → 2ZnO + 2SO₂

🔥 Calcination — carbonate ore ko hawa ki anupasthiti (kami) mein garam karna:
ZnCO₃ → ZnO + CO₂

🧠 Yaad rakhne ki trick: RoaSting = Sulphide. CalCination = Carbonate. Bas!

Step 3 — Reduction (Dhaatu Nikaalna)

Ab oxide se oxygen hataakar shuddh dhaatu nikalti hai. Tareeka dhaatu ki reactivity par depend karta hai:

ReactivityDhaatuTareeka
Sabse zyada
(K, Na, Ca, Mg, Al)		Upar waliElectrolysis — pighle hue ore mein bijli guzaarna
Madhyam
(Zn, Fe, Pb, Cu)		Beech waliCarbon (coke) se reduction
Sabse kam
(Hg, Ag, Au)		Neeche waliSirf garam karne se hi dhaatu alag ho jaati hai

✔ Examples

Carbon se reduction: ZnO + C → Zn + CO
Electrolysis (Al): 2Al₂O₃ → 4Al + 3O₂  (pighle Al₂O₃ mein bijli)
Sirf garam karke (Hg): 2HgS + 3O₂ → 2HgO + 2SO₂, phir 2HgO → 2Hg + O₂

🔥 Thermite se reduction: Kabhi-kabhi carbon ki jagah aluminium ka istemal hota hai (kyunki wo zyada reactive hai):
Fe₂O₃ + 2Al → 2Fe + Al₂O₃ + garmi — wahi thermite reaction jo railway patri jodne mein kaam aata hai!

Step 4 — Refining (Shuddhikaran)

Nikli hui dhaatu mein abhi bhi thodi milaawat hoti hai. Sabse aam tareeka hai electrolytic refining:

⚡ Anode: ashuddh (impure) dhaatu | Cathode: shuddh dhaatu ki patli patti | Electrolyte: us dhaatu ke salt ka ghol
Bijli chalane par anode se dhaatu ghulti hai aur cathode par shuddh jam jaati hai. Milaawat neeche anode mud ke roop mein baith jaati hai — jisme aksar keemti sona-chandi milti hai!

🇮🇳 India Ki Dhaatu Kahani

🇮🇳 India Connection

Odisha, Jharkhand aur Chhattisgarh Bharat ke sabse bade iron ore bhandaar hain, aur Odisha-Gujarat mein bauxite. Jamshedpur (TATA Steel, 1907) Bharat ka pehla bada steel plant tha. Aaj Bharat duniya ka doosra sabse bada steel utpadak hai. Aur Zawar (Rajasthan) ki zinc khaanein duniya ki sabse purani zinc smelting sites mein se hain — wahan 800 saal pehle hi zinc nikaalne ki takneek thi, jab baaki duniya ko pata bhi nahi tha!
