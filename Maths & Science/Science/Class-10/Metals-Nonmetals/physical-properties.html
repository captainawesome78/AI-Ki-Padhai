Duniya Do Hisson Mein Bati Hai ⚖️

Aapke aas-paas ki har cheez kisi na kisi tatva (element) se bani hai — aur lagbhag saare tatva do bade khemon mein aate hain: dhaatu (metals) aur adhaatu (non-metals). Chamakti, kadi, taar banne wali cheezein dhaatu hain; aur bhurbhuri, madham, bijli na chalane wali cheezein adhaatu.

📌 Aaj tak gyaat ~118 tatvon mein se lagbhag 90+ dhaatuein hain aur sirf ~22 adhaatuein. Yani duniya mein dhaatuein hi zyada hain!

🥇 Sone Ki Hairaan Kar Dene Wali Malleability

Sona duniya ka sabse malleable aur ductile dhaatu hai! Sirf 1 gram sone ko peet kar 1 square metre ki chaadar banayi ja sakti hai — itni patli ki uske aar-paar dikhe. Aur usi 1 gram se lagbhag 2 kilometre lambi taar kheenchi ja sakti hai! Isiliye mandiron aur kitaabon par sone ka warq (gold leaf) chadhaya jaata hai — bahut kam sone mein bahut zyada area.

⚠️ Apwaad (Exceptions) — Board Ka Favourite!

Har niyam ke apwaad hote hain, aur board exam mein yahi poocha jaata hai:

🔸 Mercury (Hg) — ek dhaatu, par kamre ke taapmaan par drav (liquid)!
🔸 Bromine (Br) — ek adhaatu, par drav!
🔸 Sodium (Na) aur Potassium (K) — dhaatuein, par itni naram ki chaaku se kat jaati hain!
🔸 Graphite — ek adhaatu, par bijli conduct karta hai aur chamakta hai!
🔸 Iodine — ek adhaatu, par chamakdaar (lustrous) hai!
🔸 Diamond — carbon (adhaatu) ka roop, par duniya ka sabse kada padarth!

✏️ Pencil Aur Heera — Ek Hi Cheez!

Aapki pencil ka "lead" (graphite) aur duniya ka sabse mehnga heera (diamond) — dono sirf carbon se bane hain! Farak kya hai? Sirf atoms ki goothbandhi. Diamond mein har carbon 4 doosre carbons se majbooti se juda hai — isliye wo itna kada hai. Graphite mein carbon parton (layers) mein hai jo ek doosre par phisal jaati hain — isliye wo kagaz par nishaan chhod deta hai aur naram lagta hai. Ek hi tatva, do bilkul alag duniyaein!

⚠️ Sodium aur potassium ko hamesha mitti ke tel (kerosene) mein dubokar rakha jaata hai. Kyun? Kyunki ye hawa ke sampark mein aate hi itni tezi se react karte hain ki aag lag jaati hai. Inhe kabhi nange haath mat chhuna.

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat sadiyon se dhaatu-vidya (metallurgy) mein aage raha hai. Wootz steel — jo dakshin Bharat mein banta tha — duniya bhar mein mashhoor tha aur usi se mashhoor "Damascus" talwarein banti thi, jo itni majboot aur dhaardaar hoti thi ki Europe ke lohaar hairaan reh jaate the. Ye Bharat ki 2000 saal purani engineering thi.
