Dhaatuon Ki "Ranking" 📊

Sab dhaatuein ek jaisi reactive nahi hoti. Potassium paani mein aag pakad leta hai, jabki sona hazaron saal tak waisa ka waisa rehta hai. Dhaatuon ko unki reactivity ke hisaab se upar se neeche sajaane par jo list banti hai, use Reactivity Series (activity series) kehte hain.

📊 Reactivity Series (upar = sabse reactive):

K > Na > Ca > Mg > Al > Zn > Fe > Pb > [H] > Cu > Hg > Ag > Au

🧠 Yaad Karne Ki Trick

"Kehti Nani Cal Mgar Aloo Znindagi Fel Pbhi Hai, Cuch Hg Agar Aur"

Ya English mein: "Please Stop Calling Me A Zebra, I Love Her Cute Silver Gold"
Koi bhi trick chuno — par ye order yaad karna zaroori hai, poore chapter mein kaam aayega!

🔑 Series Se Kya Pata Chalta Hai?

SawaalSeries se jawaab
Kaunsi dhaatu paani se react karegi?K, Na, Ca (thande paani se); Mg (garam); Al, Zn, Fe (bhaap se)
Kaunsi dhaatu acid se H₂ degi?Jo H ke upar hai (K se Pb tak)
Kaunsi dhaatu doosri ko hata degi?Jo upar hai wo neeche wale ko hataa degi
Prakriti mein shuddh (free) kaunsi milti hai?Sabse neeche wali — Au, Ag, Pt (kam reactive)
Extract karna sabse mushkil kis ka?Sabse upar wali — K, Na, Ca (electrolysis chahiye)

🔁 Displacement Reactions

📌 Niyam: Series mein jo dhaatu upar hai, wo neeche wali dhaatu ko uske compound se hata (displace) sakti hai. Ulta kabhi nahi hoga.

Reactivity series — upar wali dhaatu neeche wali ko uske salt se hata deti hai.

✔ Hoga (upar wali ne neeche wali ko hataya)

Fe + CuSO₄ → FeSO₄ + Cu  (Fe, Cu se upar hai ✓)
Neela ghol hara ho jaata hai aur keel par bhoori copper ki parat jam jaati hai.

Zn + CuSO₄ → ZnSO₄ + Cu  (Zn, Cu se upar ✓)
Cu + 2AgNO₃ → Cu(NO₃)₂ + 2Ag  (Cu, Ag se upar ✓)
Ghol neela ho jaata hai aur chandi ke chamakdaar crystals jam jaate hain.

✘ Nahi Hoga (neeche wali upar wali ko nahi hata sakti)

Cu + FeSO₄ → koi reaction nahi  (Cu, Fe se neeche hai ✗)
Ag + CuSO₄ → koi reaction nahi  (Ag, Cu se neeche ✗)

🔥 Thermite Reaction — Ek Zabardast Udaharan

Aluminium, iron se zyada reactive hai. Jab aluminium powder aur iron(III) oxide ko milakar jalaya jaata hai, toh itni zabardast garmi nikalti hai ki loha pighal jaata hai!

📐 Fe₂O₃ + 2Al → 2Fe + Al₂O₃ + bahut zyada garmi

🚂 Railway Ki Patri Kaise Jodte Hain?

Railway ki tooti hui patriyon (rails) aur bade machine parts ko jodne ke liye thermite welding istemal hoti hai. Aluminium powder aur iron oxide ka mishran patri ke joad par rakha jaata hai aur jalaya jaata hai — reaction se nikla pighla hua loha seedha darar mein bhar jaata hai aur thanda hokar patri ko jod deta hai! Bina bijli ke, kahin bhi, khule maidan mein — bas ek chemical reaction se. Aapka padha hua displacement reaction, Bharatiya Railway chala raha hai.

💎 Prakriti Mein Dhaatuein Kaise Milti Hain

ReactivityPrakriti mein kaise milti haiUdaharan
Kam reactive (neeche)Shuddh/free roop meinSona, chandi, platinum
Madhyam (beech)Oxide, sulphide, carbonate ke roop meinZn, Fe, Pb, Cu
Zyada reactive (upar)Hamesha compounds mein, kabhi shuddh nahiK, Na, Ca, Mg, Al

💡 Isiliye sona hazaron saal pehle bhi mil jaata tha (wo shuddh milta hai), par aluminium — jo Prithvi ki crust mein sabse zyada paayi jaane wali dhaatu hai — sirf 1800s mein nikala ja saka! Itna reactive hai ki use compound se alag karna bahut mushkil tha. Ek zamane mein aluminium sone se mehnga tha!
