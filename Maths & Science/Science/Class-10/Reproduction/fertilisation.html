Do Cells Se Ek Nayi Zindagi 🧬

📌 Fertilisation (nivechan): Nar yugmak (sperm) aur maada yugmak (ovum) ka milkar ek ho jaana. Isse bani nayi cell ko ZYGOTE kehte hain.

Manushya mein ye fallopian tube mein hota hai.

🔢 Chromosome Ka Hisaab — Sabse Zaroori Baat

Har manushya cell mein 46 chromosomes (23 jode) hote hain. Ab sochne wali baat: agar sperm aur ovum dono mein 46-46 hote, toh zygote mein 92 ho jaate! Aur agli peedhi mein 184... ye toh badhta hi chala jaata.

Meiosis gametes mein chromosome AADHE kar deta hai — isiliye har peedhi mein sankhya 46 hi rehti hai.

✅ Prakriti ka hal: Gametes (sperm aur ovum) banti hain ek khaas cell division se jise MEIOSIS kehte hain, jisme chromosome aadhe (half) ho jaate hain.

Sperm = 23  +  Ovum = 23  →  Zygote = 46 ✓

Isi wajah se har peedhi mein chromosome ki sankhya sthir rehti hai!

💡 Aur yahi wajah hai ki bachche mein aadhe gun maa se aur aadhe pita se aate hain — isiliye wo dono jaisa lagta hai, par kisi ek ki hubahu copy nahi hota.

👶 Bhroon Ka Vikas

1️⃣ Zygote banta hai (fallopian tube mein)
2️⃣ Wo baar-baar bat'ta hai aur ek gola (embryo) ban jaata hai
3️⃣ Embryo uterus mein pahunch kar uski moti parat mein chipak jaata hai — ise implantation kehte hain
4️⃣ Dheere-dheere ang bante hain — ab ise foetus kehte hain
5️⃣ Lagbhag 9 mahine baad bachche ka janm

🫀 Placenta (aanvel): Ek khaas ang jo maa aur bachche ko jodta hai.
➡️ Maa se bachche ko: poshan aur oxygen
⬅️ Bachche se maa ko: kachra (waste) jise maa ke gurde saaf karte hain

Placenta mein bahut saare ubhaar (villi) hote hain taaki aadan-pradaan ka area zyada ho.

🤰 Garbhavastha mein maa ka swasthya: Garbhvati mahila ko poshak khaana (iron, calcium, protein), aaram aur samay-samay par doctor se jaanch chahiye. Sharaab, tambaakoo aur bina doctor ki salaah ke dawaiyan bachche ko nuksaan pahunchati hain.

Bharat mein sarkari aspatalon mein garbhvati mahilaon ke liye muft jaanch aur prasav (delivery) ki suvidha hai.

👦👧 Ladka Ya Ladki — Kaise Tay Hota Hai?

Manushya ke 23 jodon mein se ek joda ling gunsutra (sex chromosomes) kehlata hai:

👩 Maa: XX → uske saare ande mein hamesha X
👨 Pita: XY → uske aadhe sperm mein X, aadhe mein Y

Agar X wala sperm mila → XX → LADKI
Agar Y wala sperm mila → XY → LADKA

⚠️ Bahut zaroori baat: Iska matlab hai ki bachche ka ling PITA ke sperm se tay hota hai — maa ka isme koi haath nahi hota!

Bharat mein aaj bhi kai jagah ladki paida hone par maa ko dosh diya jaata hai — ye vaigyanik roop se bilkul galat hai. Aur ye vigyan padhna hi is galat soch ko todta hai.

⚖️ Kanoon: Bharat mein janm se pehle ling ki jaanch (sex determination) kanoonan apradh hai (PCPNDT Act, 1994). Iska maqsad kanya bhroon hatya (female foeticide) rokna hai. "Beti Bachao Beti Padhao" jaise abhiyan isi liye chalaye jaate hain.

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat mein ling anupat (sex ratio) — yani 1000 ladkon par kitni ladkiyan — kai rajyon mein chintajanak raha hai. Kanoon, jaagrukta aur shiksha se ismein sudhaar aa raha hai. Ek vaigyanik sach jaanna (ki ling pita se tay hota hai) laakhon betiyon ki zindagi bacha sakta hai.
