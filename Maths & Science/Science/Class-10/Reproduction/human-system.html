Javaani (Puberty) — Shareer Taiyaar Hota Hai 🌱

Bachpan mein prajanan tantra bana toh hota hai, par kaam nahi karta. Lagbhag 10-14 saal ki umar mein hormones sakriya hote hain aur shareer mein badlav aane lagte hain — is umar ko javaani (puberty) kehte hain.

💚 Yaad rakho: Ye saare badlav bilkul saamanya aur swabhavik hain, aur har vyakti mein alag umar par aate hain — kisi mein jaldi, kisi mein der se. Isme sharm ya ghabrane ki koi baat nahi hai. Koi bhi sawaal ho toh apne maa-baap, badi behen-bhai, teacher ya doctor se poochna bilkul theek hai.

🔬 Sperm bahut chhota hota hai — usme lagbhag sirf genetic material aur ek lambi poonch (tail) hoti hai jisse wo tair sake.

🔬 Ladki ke janm ke samay hi uski ovaries mein hazaron ande maujood hote hain. Javaani ke baad, lagbhag har 28 din mein ek ovary se ek anda nikalta hai — ise ovulation kehte hain.

Female prajanan tantra — ovum ovary se nikal kar fallopian tube mein pahunchta hai, jahan fertilisation hota hai.

🩸 Maasik Dharm (Menstruation)

Har mahine uterus ki andaruni parat moti aur mulayam ho jaati hai — taaki agar fertilisation ho toh bhroon usme aaram se chipak sake.

➡️ Agar fertilisation ho jaaye — bhroon uterus ki parat mein chipak jaata hai aur badhne lagta hai.

➡️ Agar fertilisation na ho — us moti parat ki zaroorat nahi rehti, isliye wo toot kar khoon ke saath bahar nikal jaati hai. Isi ko maasik dharm (periods) kehte hain. Ye lagbhag 2-8 din chalta hai aur har ~28 din mein dohraya jaata hai.

💚 Maasik dharm ek bilkul saamanya aur swasth prakriya hai — ye koi bimari ya "gandi" cheez nahi hai. Ise lekar jo bhram aur sharm samaaj mein hai, wo galat hai.

🩺 Dhyan rakhne ki baatein: saaf sanitary pad ya cloth istemal karein aur use har 4-6 ghante mein badlein, haath dhoyein, khoob paani piyein, aur iron wala khaana (palak, gud, chana) khaayein kyunki khoon ke saath iron nikalta hai. Bahut zyada dard ya bahut zyada rakt-srav ho toh doctor se milna chahiye.

👨‍👩‍👧 Ladkon ko bhi ye samajhna chahiye — taaki wo mazaak na udaayein balki samajhdaari dikhayein.

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat mein maasik swachhta (menstrual hygiene) par kaafi kaam hua hai. Sarkar ki yojanaon aur "Padman" jaise abhiyanon se sasta sanitary pad gaon tak pahuncha hai. Phir bhi kai jagah is par baat karna "varjit (taboo)" maana jaata hai, jisse ladkiyan school chhod deti hain. Sahi jaankari hi is bhram ko todti hai — isiliye ye chapter padhna zaroori hai.
