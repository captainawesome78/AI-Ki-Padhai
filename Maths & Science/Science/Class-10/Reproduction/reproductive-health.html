Swasthya Ka Matlab Sirf Bimari Na Hona Nahi 🩺

Vishwa Swasthya Sangathan (WHO) ke anusaar swasthya ka matlab hai shaaririk, maansik aur samajik — teenon roop se theek hona. Prajanan swasthya iska ek zaroori hissa hai, aur ise samajhna har kishor (teenager) ka adhikaar hai.

💚 Is lesson ki jaankari vaigyanik aur swasthya ke nazariye se hai — jaisa NCERT syllabus mein diya gaya hai. Iska maqsad hai ki aapko sahi tathya milein, kyunki galat jaankari aur afwaahein hi sabse zyada nuksaan karti hain.

👨‍👩‍👧 Parivar Niyojan (Family Planning)

Parivar niyojan ka matlab hai — soch-samajh kar tay karna ki kitne bachche hon aur kab hon.

Contraception ke chaar prakaar — barrier, hormonal, IUCD aur surgical.

📌 Zaroori kyun hai?
1️⃣ Maa ka swasthya — bahut jaldi-jaldi ya bahut kam umar mein garbhdhaaran maa ke liye khatarnaak hai
2️⃣ Bachche ka swasthya — har bachche ko poora poshan aur dhyan mil sake
3️⃣ Jansankhya (population) par niyantran — desh ke sansaadhan seemit hain

TareekaKaise kaam karta hai
Barrier methods (condom)Sperm ko ande tak pahunchne se rokte hain. Ye ekmatra tareeka hai jo STD/HIV se bhi bachata hai
Hormonal methods (goli/injection)Hormone badal kar ovulation (ande ka nikalna) rokte hain
IUCD (Copper-T)Uterus mein rakha jaata hai, jisse implantation nahi ho paata
Surgical methodsSthayi (permanent) — nar mein vasectomy, maada mein tubectomy

⚠️ Koi bhi tareeka apnane se pehle doctor ki salaah zaroori hai — kyunki har vyakti ke liye alag tareeka theek hota hai, aur kuch ke side-effects bhi ho sakte hain.

🦠 Sankramak Rog (STDs) Aur HIV/AIDS

Kuch bimariyan ek vyakti se doosre mein yaun sampark (sexual contact) se failti hain — inhe STD (Sexually Transmitted Diseases) kehte hain.

BimariKaaran
Gonorrhoea, SyphilisBacteria — antibiotics se ilaaj sambhav
Warts, HIV/AIDSVirus — HIV ka abhi tak poora ilaaj nahi

🦠 HIV kya karta hai? Ye virus shareer ki rog-pratirodhak kshamata (immune system) ki WBC ko hi kamzor kar deta hai. Isse shareer choti-choti bimariyon se bhi ladne layak nahi rehta — is aakhri avastha ko AIDS kehte hain.

✅ HIV ISSE failta hai❌ HIV ISSE NAHI failta
Asurakshit yaun samparkHaath milane ya gale lagne se
Sankramit khoon chadhane seSaath khaana khaane se
Ek hi soi (needle/syringe) baar-baar istemal karne seEk hi bartan ya toilet istemal karne se
Sankramit maa se bachche koMachhar ke kaatne se

💚 Ye jaanna bahut zaroori hai: HIV se peedit vyakti ke saath baithna, khaana, padhna aur dosti karna poori tarah surakshit hai. Unse door bhaagna ya bhedbhav karna vaigyanik roop se galat aur amanviya hai. Aaj dawaiyon (ART) se HIV peedit log lambi aur swasth zindagi jee sakte hain.

🛡️ Bachav: hamesha nayi aur sterile soi ka istemal, jaanchaa hua khoon hi chadhwana, aur sahi jaankari rakhna.

👩 Mahilaon Ka Swasthya Aur Baal Vivah

⚠️ Kam umar mein garbhdhaaran khatarnaak kyun hai? Kishoravastha (teenage) mein ladki ka shareer abhi poori tarah vikasit nahi hota. Is umar mein garbh se maa aur bachche dono ki jaan ko khatra hota hai, khoon ki kami (anaemia) hoti hai, aur ladki ki padhai chhoot jaati hai.

⚖️ Isiliye Bharat mein kanoon hai: vivah ki nyoontam aayu ladki ke liye 18 saal aur ladke ke liye 21 saal hai. Baal vivah kanoonan apradh hai.

📌 Kishoron ke liye zaroori baatein:
✅ Sahi jaankari lena sharm ki baat nahi — afwaah se hamesha behtar hai
✅ Apne shareer par sirf aapka adhikaar hai — kisi bhi galat sparsh ya dabaav ko NA kehna aapka poora haq hai
✅ Kuch bhi asahaj lage toh maa-baap, teacher ya doctor se zaroor baat karein
✅ Bharat mein bachchon ke liye CHILDLINE 1098 par muft madad milti hai

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat sarkar ke Rashtriya Kishor Swasthya Karyakram (RKSK) ke tahat kishoron ke liye aspatalon mein alag salaah kendra hote hain, jahan gopniya (confidential) aur muft salaah milti hai. Aur "Beti Bachao Beti Padhao" jaise abhiyan ladkiyon ki shiksha aur swasthya dono par kaam karte hain — kyunki padhi-likhi ladki ka poora parivar swasth rehta hai.

📖 Chapter Summary

✅ Prajanan prajaati ko bachata hai; DNA copy mein variation aati hai jo evolution ka aadhaar hai
✅ Asexual: fission, budding, fragmentation, regeneration, spore, vegetative propagation
✅ Phool: pollination → pollen tube → fertilisation; ovary→phal, ovule→beej
✅ Manushya: testes (sperm+testosterone), ovary (ovum+oestrogen); fertilisation fallopian tube mein
✅ Gametes mein 23, zygote mein 46 chromosomes; ling PITA ke sperm (X/Y) se tay
✅ Parivar niyojan aur STD/HIV se bachav; baal vivah kanoonan apradh
