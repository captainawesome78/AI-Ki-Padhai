Reproduction Kyun Zaroori Hai 🌱

Ek Ajeeb Sawaal 🤔

Saans lena, khaana khaana, khoon ka behna — ye sab ek jeev ke apne zinda rehne ke liye zaroori hai. Par reproduction (prajanan)? Agar koi jeev bachche paida na kare, tab bhi wo khud toh zinda reh sakta hai! Phir reproduction ko "life process" kyun kehte hain?

📌 Kyunki reproduction ek vyakti (individual) ke liye nahi, poori species (prajaati) ke liye zaroori hai. Har jeev ek din marta hai — agar naye jeev na banein toh poori species hi khatam ho jaayegi.

🧬 Asli Kahani — DNA Ki Copy

Reproduction ki jad mein ek hi kaam hai: DNA ki nakal (copy) banana. DNA mein hi wo saara "nirdesh" likha hai jo tay karta hai ki jeev kaisa dikhega aur kaise kaam karega.

📌 Cell pehle apne DNA ki copy banati hai, phir do hisson mein bat jaati hai — taaki dono nayi cells ko poora nirdesh mil jaaye.

🎲 Variation — Chhoti Galtiyon Ka Bada Faayda

DNA copy karte waqt kabhi-kabhi chhoti galtiyan ho jaati hain. Isse naya jeev apne parent se bilkul same nahi hota — thoda alag hota hai. Isi antar ko variation (vividhata) kehte hain.

Jab sab ek jaise hon toh ek hi aafat sabko khatam kar deti hai. Variation species ka beema hai.
🦠 Garam Paani Wali Kahani

Socho ek talab mein hazaron bacteria hain — sab bilkul ek jaise. Achanak paani ka taapmaan bahut badh jaata hai. Agar sab bilkul ek jaise hote, toh sab ke sab mar jaate — species khatam!

Par maano kuch bacteria mein DNA ki chhoti galti se garmi sehne ki kshamata aa gayi thi. Ab wahi kuch bacteria bach jaayenge aur aage badhenge. Yani jo cheez "galti" lagti thi, wahi species ki jaan bachane wali nikli!

Isiliye kehte hain: variation hi species ka beema (insurance) hai.

📌 Variation ke do bade faayde:
1️⃣ Badalte maahol (temperature, bimari, khaane ki kami) mein kuch na kuch bach jaate hain
2️⃣ Yahi variation lambe samay mein evolution ka aadhaar banti hai

⚖️ Par bahut zyada variation bhi theek nahi! Agar DNA copy mein bahut galtiyan ho jaayein toh cell hi kaam karna band kar degi. Isiliye prakriti mein santulan (balance) hai — copy zyadatar sahi hoti hai, galtiyan bahut kam.

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat mein kele (banana) ki kheti lagbhag poori tarah vegetative propagation (asexual) se hoti hai — isliye ek khet ke saare kele genetically ek jaise (clone) hote hain. Faayda: har phal ek jaisa aur meetha. Par khatra bhi bada — agar koi ek bimari aa jaaye toh poori fasal khatam ho sakti hai, kyunki kisi mein bhi ladne ki alag kshamata nahi hai. Yahi variation ki kami ka nuksaan hai.
