Hamare Pairon Ke Neeche Kya Hai? 🕳️

Agar aap ek bahut gehra gaddha khodte jaayein, toh kya milega? Pehle mitti, phir chattan, phir... garam pighla hua patthar, aur aakhir mein lohe ka ek dahakta hua gola! Prithvi ek thos gend nahi hai - ye pyaaz (onion) ki tarah kai parton (layers) se bani hai. Is poore thos hisse ko geosphere kehte hain.

⛏️ Duniya Ka Sabse Gehra Ched

Russia ke vaigyanikon ne 1970 mein ek prayog shuru kiya - Prithvi mein jitna ho sake utna gehra khodna (Kola Superdeep Borehole). 20 saal ki mehnat ke baad wo sirf 12.2 kilometre tak pahunch paaye - phir garmi (180°C se zyada) ne drill hi kharaab kar di! Aur Prithvi ki gehrai hai 6,371 km. Yani insaan ne aaj tak Prithvi ki sirf 0.2% gehrai chhui hai. Andar ki baaki jaankari hume bhukamp (earthquake) waves se milti hai!

🧅 Prithvi Ki Teen Mukhya Layers

Prithvi ki layers - patli crust se lekar dahakte inner core tak.

1. Crust (Bhupatal) - Sabse Upar Ki Patli Parat

Ye hamari "zameen" hai jispar hum rehte hain - lagbhag 5 km (samundar ke neeche) se 70 km (pahaadon ke neeche) tak moti. Prithvi ke poore size ke hisaab se ye itni patli hai jaise seb (apple) ka chhilka! Isi mein mitti, chattanein, pahaad aur saari zindagi hai.

2. Mantle (Prawar) - Sabse Mota Hissa

Crust ke neeche lagbhag 2,900 km moti parat. Yahan chattan itni garam hai ki wo dheere-dheere behti hai (jaise bahut gaadha shahad). Isi ke behne se upar ki plates khiskti hain - aur wahi bhukamp aur jwalamukhi ka kaaran banta hai.

3. Core (Kendra) - Lohe Ka Dahakta Gola

Sabse andar do hissa hai:

LayerHalatKhaas baat
Outer corePighla hua (liquid) loha-nickelIske behne se Prithvi ka magnetic field banta hai
Inner coreThos (solid) lohaTemperature ~5,500°C - lagbhag suraj ki satah jitna garam! Bahut zyada pressure ki wajah se pighla nahi
🧭 Compass Kaam Kyun Karta Hai?

Prithvi ke outer core mein pighla hua loha lagataar ghoomta rehta hai, jisse Prithvi ke charon taraf ek vishaal magnetic field ban jaata hai. Isi ki wajah se compass ki sui hamesha uttar (north) dikhati hai - aur isi field ki wajah se suraj se aane wali khatarnaak radiation se hum surakshit rehte hain. Prithvi ke andar ka loha hume bacha raha hai!

🕌 Kya Aap Jaante Hain?

Taj Mahal safed marble (sangmarmar) se bana hai - jo ek metamorphic rock hai. Ye kabhi limestone thi, jo Prithvi ke andar zabardast garmi aur dabaav se marble ban gayi. Rajasthan ke Makrana se ye marble laakar Agra pahunchaya gaya tha. Duniya ki sabse khoobsurat imaarat ke peeche geosphere ki lakhon saal ki mehnat hai!

🧩 Plates Aur Bhukamp

Prithvi ki crust ek poori chaadar nahi hai - ye bade-bade tukdon (tectonic plates) mein bati hui hai, jo mantle par bahut dheere-dheere khiskte rehte hain (saal mein kuch centimetre - jitni tezi se aapke nakhun badhte hain!). Jab ye plates takraati, ragadti ya door hoti hain, tab:

🌋 Jwalamukhi (volcano) phatte hain
🫨 Bhukamp (earthquake) aate hain
⛰️ Pahaad bante hain

🇮🇳 India Connection

Himalaya duniya ka sabse ooncha pahaad isliye hai kyunki Indian plate lagataar Eurasian plate se takra rahi hai! Lagbhag 5 crore saal pehle India ek alag dweep tha jo uttar ki taraf khiskkar Asia se takraya - aur us takkar se Himalaya upar uth gaya. Aur sabse hairaan karne wali baat - Himalaya aaj bhi har saal lagbhag 5 mm badh raha hai, kyunki plates abhi bhi dhakka de rahi hain!

🌱 Mitti (Soil) - Geosphere Ka Sabse Keemti Hissa

Mitti sirf "dhool" nahi hai - ye chattanon ke tootne (weathering) se banti hai aur isme jeev, hawa, paani aur sade-gale padarth (humus) sab hote hain. Ek centimetre mitti banne mein sainkdon saal lag sakte hain, par kharab hone mein kuch hi saal!

🌾 Soil erosion (mitti ka kataav) aaj ki badi samasya hai - ped kaatne, zyada charai aur galat kheti se upari upjaau mitti bah jaati hai. Isse bachne ke liye ped lagana, contour farming aur fasal badalna (crop rotation) zaroori hai.
