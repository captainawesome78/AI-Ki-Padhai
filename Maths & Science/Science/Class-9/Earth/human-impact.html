Ek Prajaati, Poora Grah 👣

Prithvi 460 crore saal purani hai. Insaan yahan sirf kuch laakh saal se hai - Prithvi ki umar ke hisaab se ek pal jitna. Phir bhi, is chhote se samay mein humne is grah ke chaaron spheres ko badal diya hai: hawa ki banawat, nadiyon ka bahaav, jungle, mitti, aur mausam tak. Koi doosri prajaati ne aisa nahi kiya.

📌 Ab tak humne dekha ki Prithvi ke spheres ek doosre se jude hain. Iska ek gehra matlab ye bhi hai - ek sphere mein badlav baaki sab par asar daalta hai. Isiliye insaani gatividhiyon ka asar sirf ek jagah nahi rukta.

😷 Sardiyon mein uttar Bharat ke kai shehron mein smog chha jaata hai - dhuaan aur kohre ka khatarnaak mishran. Ye khaas taur par bachchon, buzurgon aur dama ke mareezon ke liye nuksaandeh hai. Aise dinon mein bahar zyada mehnat wali kasrat se bachna aur mask lagana behtar hota hai.

🐟 Plastic Ka Safar

Aapne jo plastic ki bottle aaj phenki, wo 450 saal tak nasht nahi hogi! Samundar mein pahunchkar plastic bahut chhote tukdon (microplastics) mein toot jaata hai, jise machhliyan kha leti hain - aur wahi machhli hamari thali tak wapas aa jaati hai. Yani hamara phenka hua kachra ghoomkar hum tak hi laut aata hai. Ye Earth system ka sabse seedha sabak hai.

🌡️ Global Warming Aur Climate Change

Pichle lesson mein humne greenhouse effect padha - jo apne aap mein zaroori hai. Par jab hum koyla, petrol aur diesel jalate hain, toh hawa mein CO₂ zaroorat se zyada badh jaati hai. Kambal mota ho jaata hai, aur Prithvi zyada garam hone lagti hai - ise global warming kehte hain.

📌 Global warming = Prithvi ke average temperature ka badhna.
Climate change = Us garmi ki wajah se mausam ke poore tareeke (pattern) ka badalna - baarish, sookha, toofan, sab.

🏔️ Himalaya Ke Glacier

Himalaya ko "teesra dhruv (Third Pole)" kehte hain kyunki Antarctica aur Arctic ke baad sabse zyada barf yahin hai. Ganga, Yamuna aur Brahmaputra jaisi nadiyaan inhi glaciers se paani paati hain, jinpar karodon log nirbhar hain. Global warming se ye glacier pighal rahe hain - isliye climate change Bharat ke liye sirf ek "vaigyanik baat" nahi, seedhe paani aur khaane ka sawaal hai.

🌳 Deforestation Aur Jaiv Vividhata Ka Nuksaan

Kheti, shehar aur udyog ke liye jungle kaate ja rahe hain. Par ped sirf lakdi nahi dete - wo CO₂ soakte hain, oxygen dete hain, mitti ko pakadte hain, baarish laate hain aur laakhon jeevon ka ghar hain. Jungle khatm hone se ye sab ek saath khatm hota hai.

🌲 Chipko Andolan

1973 mein Uttarakhand ke gaon walon ne - jinme zyadatar mahilaayein thi, jaise Gaura Devi - pedon ko kaatne se bachane ke liye unhe gale laga liya (chipak gaye). Unhone kaha - "pehle hume kaato, phir ped ko". Bina kisi hinsa ke, is Chipko Andolan ne sarkar ko pedon ki kataai par rok lagane par majboor kar diya. Ye duniya ke sabse mashhoor paryavaran andolanon mein se ek bana - aur saabit kiya ki aam log bhi bada badlav la sakte hain.

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat duniya ke sabse bade solar energy programs chala raha hai aur International Solar Alliance ka sansthapak (founder) desh hai. Iske alawa Swachh Bharat Abhiyan, single-use plastic par rok, aur ujjwala/LED jaise programs paryavaran ki disha mein bade kadam hain. Par sabse zaroori hissa aap hain - kyunki aadatein gharon se badalti hain.

🌱 Aakhri Baat

Is poore chapter mein humne dekha - mitti, paani, hawa aur jeev alag-alag nahi, ek hi system ke hisse hain. Aur us system mein aap bhi shaamil ho. Aap jo saans lete ho wo kisi ped ne banayi, jo paani peete ho wo kabhi badal tha, aur aapke shareer ke atoms kabhi taaron mein bane the. Prithvi ko bachana koi "doosron ka kaam" nahi - ye apne hi ghar ki dekhbhaal hai.
