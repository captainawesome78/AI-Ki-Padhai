Bijli Pehle, Gadgadaahat Baad Mein ⚡

Baarish mein aasman mein bijli (lightning) chamakti hai, aur kuch second baad "gadgadahat" (thunder) sunai deti hai. Par bijli aur gadgadahat dono ek saath hi to hue the! Phir aawaz der se kyun aayi? Kyunki light (roshni) sound se laakhon guna tez chalti hai. Roshni turant aa jaati hai, par sound ko aap tak pahunchne mein time lagta hai. Isi se pata chalta hai - sound ki apni ek speed hai, aur wo utni tez nahi hai.

📌 Hawa mein (20°C par) sound ki speed lagbhag 340 m/s hoti hai - yani ek second mein lagbhag 340 metre. Light ki speed 30 crore m/s hai (lagbhag 9 lakh guna tez)!

⚡ Toofan Kitni Door Hai? (Mazedaar Trick)

Jab bijli chamke, seconds ginna shuru karo jab tak gadgadahat na sunai de. Har 3 second ka matlab toofan lagbhag 1 kilometre door hai (kyunki sound 1 km jaane mein ~3 s leti hai). Agar 9 second gine, toh toofan ~3 km door hai. Ye physics ka rozmarra istemal hai!

🧱 Sound Kis Medium Mein Sabse Tez?

Sound ki speed medium (maadhyam) par bahut depend karti hai. Ye tab tez chalti hai jab particles paas-paas hon, taaki wo jaldi ek doosre ko dhakka de sakein. Isliye:

📌 Solid (thos) > Liquid (drav) > Gas
Sound thos mein sabse tez, gas (jaise hawa) mein sabse dheemi chalti hai.

Loha / steel (iron)~5100 m/s
Kaanch (glass)~5000 m/s
🚂 Railway Track Wali Purani Trick

Purane zamaane mein log railway track (loha) par kaan lagakar door aa rahi train ka pata laga lete the - kyunki lohe mein sound hawa se kai guna tez pahunchti hai! Aap track ke through train ki aawaz tab hi sun lete jab wo hawa ke through abhi door thi. (Yaad rahe - aaj ye khatarnaak hai, kabhi na karein!)

🌡️ Temperature Ka Asar

Sound ki speed medium ke temperature par bhi depend karti hai. Garam hawa mein particles tezi se hilte hain, isliye sound zyada tez chalti hai. Isliye garmi ke din sound thodi tez, aur sardi mein thodi dheemi chalti hai.

📌 Temperature badhne par gas mein sound ki speed badhti hai. Isiliye "340 m/s" ek approx value hai jo temperature ke saath thodi badalti rehti hai.

✈️ Supersonic aur Sonic Boom

Jab koi cheez (jaise fighter jet) sound ki speed se bhi tez udti hai, use supersonic kehte hain. Iski speed ko Mach number mein naapte hain:

📐 Mach number = object ki speed / sound ki speed
Mach 1 = sound ki speed. Mach 2 = sound se dugni tez. Mach 1 se kam = subsonic, zyada = supersonic.

Jab jet sound ki speed paar karta hai, toh wo hawa mein bani sound waves ko "peeche chhod" deta hai aur ye waves ek saath ikatthi hokar ek zabardast shock wave banati hain. Zameen par ye ek dhamaake jaisi tez aawaz - sonic boom - ke roop mein sunai deti hai. Kabhi-kabhi ye itni tez hoti hai ki khidkiyon ke kaanch hil jaate hain!

✈️ Kya Aap Jaante Hain?

Concorde (ek mashhoor supersonic passenger plane) London se New York sirf ~3.5 ghante mein pahunch jaata tha - normal plane se aadhe se bhi kam time! Wo Mach 2 (sound se dugni speed) par udta tha. Sonic boom ki wajah se supersonic planes ko shehron ke upar dheere udna padta hai.

🧮 Solved Numericals

✔ Example 1: Doori nikalna

Sound hawa mein 340 m/s se chalti hai. 5 second mein wo kitni door jaayegi?
Doori = speed × time = 340 × 5 = 1700 m.

✔ Example 2: Time nikalna

Ek awaaz ko 680 m door jaane mein kitna time lagega (v = 340 m/s)?
Time = doori / speed = 680 / 340 = 2 second.

✔ Example 3: Mach number

Ek jet 680 m/s se udta hai, sound ki speed 340 m/s hai. Mach number?
Mach = 680 / 340 = 2 (yani supersonic, Mach 2).

🚀 India Ki Udaan

🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat ne BrahMos supersonic cruise missile banayi hai jo Mach 2.8-3 (sound se lagbhag 3 guna tez) tak udti hai - duniya ki sabse tez cruise missiles mein se ek. India ka Tejas fighter jet bhi supersonic hai. In sabke design mein sound ki speed, shock waves aur aerodynamics ki gehri physics ka istemal hota hai - jo aap abhi seekh rahe ho!
