Energy Kabhi Khatm Nahi Hoti 🌌

Prakriti ka ek bahut khoobsurat aur atal (unbreakable) niyam hai - energy na banayi ja sakti hai, na nasht (destroy) ki ja sakti hai. Wo bas ek roop se doosre roop mein badalti rehti hai. Poori duniya mein jitni energy hai, wo hamesha utni hi rehti hai - kabhi kam-zyada nahi hoti.

📌 Law of Conservation of Energy: Energy ko na toh banaya ja sakta hai aur na hi mitaaya ja sakta hai; wo sirf ek form se doosre form mein transform hoti hai. Kisi bhi transformation mein total energy hamesha constant (sthir) rehti hai.

🎢 Roller Coaster Ki Kahani

Roller coaster ki gaadi ko pehle motor sabse oonchi choti tak le jaata hai - wahan uski potential energy sabse zyada hoti hai aur speed lagbhag zero. Phir wo neeche girti hai - PE tezi se kinetic energy mein badalti jaati hai, gaadi sabse tez neeche pahunchti hai. Phir agli chadhai par KE wapas PE ban jaati hai aur speed kam. Poore ride mein energy bas KE aur PE ke beech "adla-badli" karti rehti hai - total energy wahi rehti hai!

🔄 KE aur PE Ki Adla-Badli

Mechanical energy do hisson se milkar banti hai: Mechanical Energy = KE + PE. Jab koi friction na ho, toh ye total mechanical energy hamesha constant rehti hai - PE ghatti hai toh KE badhti hai, aur ulta.

📐 KE + PE = constant (jab air resistance / friction na ho)
½mv² + mgh = hamesha wahi total

🍎 Free Fall - Ek Girta Hua Object

Sabse saaf example ek girte hue object ka hai. Maan lo ek object oonchai par rukha hai. Jaise-jaise wo girta hai, uski oonchai (h) ghatti jaati hai (PE ghatti hai), aur speed (v) badhti jaati hai (KE badhti hai). Har point par KE + PE wahi rehta hai.

Girte waqt PE lagataar KE mein badalti hai, par KE + PE constant rehta hai.

✔ Example: 2 kg ka object 5 m se girta hai (g = 10)

Shuruaat (top): PE = mgh = 2 × 10 × 5 = 100 J, KE = 0. Total = 100 J.
Beech mein (h = 2.5 m): PE = 2 × 10 × 2.5 = 50 J, toh KE = 100 − 50 = 50 J. Total = 100 J.
Zameen par (h = 0): PE = 0, toh KE = 100 J. Total = 100 J.
Har jagah total = 100 J! Yahi conservation hai.
Zameen par speed nikaalo: KE = ½mv² → 100 = ½ × 2 × v² → v² = 100 → v = 10 m/s.

🕰️ Simple Pendulum - Aage Peeche Jhoolna

Ek jhoolta hua pendulum conservation ka perfect example hai. Extreme (kinaare) par wo pal bhar rukta hai - wahan KE = 0 aur PE maximum. Beech (lowest point) par wo sabse tez hota hai - wahan PE = 0 aur KE maximum. Ye energy aage-peeche badalti rehti hai.

🤔 Toh Pendulum Rukta Kyun Hai?

Agar energy conserve hoti hai toh pendulum humesha kyun nahi jhoolta rehta? Kyunki asli duniya mein air resistance aur friction hote hain. Ye thoda-thoda mechanical energy ko heat aur sound mein badal dete hain. Energy nasht nahi hui - bas garmi aur aawaz ban gayi! Total energy phir bhi constant hai, isiliye pendulum dheere-dheere ruk jaata hai.
