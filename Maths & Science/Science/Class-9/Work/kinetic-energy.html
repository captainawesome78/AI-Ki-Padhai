Energy Kya Hai? ⚡

Energy woh cheez hai jiski wajah se koi kaam (work) ho sakta hai. Jitni zyada energy, utna zyada kaam. Suraj ki energy se paudhe khaana banate hain, khaane ki energy se aap dauadte ho, petrol ki energy se gaadi chalti hai. Energy ke bina duniya mein kuch bhi nahi ho sakta.

📌 Energy: Kaam karne ki kshamata (capacity to do work). Energy ki SI unit bhi work jaisi hi hai - Joule (J).

Energy kai roop (forms) mein aati hai - kinetic (gati), potential (sthiti), heat, light, sound, chemical, electrical, nuclear. Is chapter mein hum do sabse important mechanical forms padhenge: Kinetic Energy aur Potential Energy. Shuruaat kinetic se.

💨 Hawa Ki Taakat

Ek halki si hawa aapke baalon ko sirf hilaati hai. Lekin wahi hawa jab toofan (storm) ban jaati hai, toh bade-bade ped ukhaad deti hai aur chhaton ko uda le jaati hai! Hawa toh wahi hai - bas uski speed badh gayi. Aur speed badhne se uski gati urja (kinetic energy) itni badh jaati hai ki wo tabaahi macha deti hai. Yahi kinetic energy ki taakat hai.

🏃 Kinetic Energy - Chalti Cheezon Ki Energy

Har chalti (moving) cheez mein energy hoti hai - isi ko Kinetic Energy (KE) ya "gati urja" kehte hain. Ek chalti cricket ball khidki tod sakti hai, chalta hua paani turbine ghuma sakta hai, chalti gaadi takraye toh nuksaan karti hai - kyunki in sab mein kinetic energy hoti hai.

📐 Kinetic Energy = ½ × mass × velocity²
KE = ½ m v²
(m = mass kilogram mein, v = velocity metre/second mein, KE Joule mein)

Is formula mein ek bahut important baat chhupi hai: velocity square (v²) mein aati hai! Iska matlab - agar speed dugni (double) ho jaaye, toh kinetic energy chaar guna (4×) ho jaati hai. Speed teen guna → energy 9 guna! Isiliye tez gaadi ke accidents itne khatarnaak hote hain.

Speed dugni hone par kinetic energy 4 guna badh jaati hai (v² ki wajah se).

🔍 KE = ½mv² Kahan Se Aaya? (Derivation)

Maan lo ek object mass m ke saath rukka hua (rest par, u = 0) hai. Uspar ek constant force F lagti hai jo usse acceleration a deti hai aur wo doori s tay karke velocity v pakad leta hai.

Force ne jo kaam kiya wahi object ki kinetic energy ban gaya:

Work done, W = F × s   ...(1)
Newton's 2nd law se: F = m × a   ...(2)
Motion ki equation se (u = 0): v² = u² + 2as = 2as  →  s = v² / 2a   ...(3)
(2) aur (3) ko (1) mein rakho:
W = (m × a) × (v² / 2a) = ½ m v²
Yahi kaam object ki Kinetic Energy hai → KE = ½mv²

Iska matlab: kisi rukhe object ko velocity v tak laane ke liye jitna kaam karna padta hai, utni hi uski kinetic energy ho jaati hai. Energy aur work ek doosre mein badalte rehte hain - isse Work-Energy Theorem kehte hain.

⚖️ Work-Energy Theorem

Kisi object par lagi net force ka kaam uski kinetic energy ke badlav (change) ke barabar hota hai: W = KE(final) − KE(initial) = ½mv² − ½mu². Yaani kaam se hi energy badalti hai.

🧮 Solved Numericals

✔ Example 1: Cricket ball

0.16 kg ki cricket ball 20 m/s se aa rahi hai. Uski kinetic energy?
KE = ½ m v² = ½ × 0.16 × (20)² = ½ × 0.16 × 400 = 32 J.

✔ Example 2: Speed double karne par

Wahi ball 40 m/s (double speed) se aaye toh?
KE = ½ × 0.16 × (40)² = ½ × 0.16 × 1600 = 128 J.
Dhyan do - speed double hui, par KE 32 se 128 yaani 4 guna ho gayi!

✔ Example 3: Bike vs Truck

150 kg ki bike 10 m/s par: KE = ½ × 150 × 100 = 7500 J.
3000 kg ka truck 10 m/s par: KE = ½ × 3000 × 100 = 150000 J.
Same speed par bhi truck ki energy 20 guna zyada - isiliye truck ka accident zyada khatarnaak.

✔ Example 4: Kaam se energy nikalna

Ek 2 kg ka object rest se chala aur 500 J kaam hone par uski speed kya hogi?
KE = ½mv² → 500 = ½ × 2 × v² → v² = 500 → v = 22.36 m/s.
