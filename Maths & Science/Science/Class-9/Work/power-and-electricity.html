Kaam To Theek, Par Kitni Jaldi? ⏱️

Do mazdoor 100 kitaabein doosri manzil par le jaate hain. Ek 5 minute mein kaam khatm karta hai, doosra 20 minute leta hai. Dono ne same kaam kiya (same energy) - lekin pehla mazdoor zyada powerful hai, kyunki usne wo kaam jaldi kiya. Yahi "power" hai - kaam karne ki speed (rate).

📌 Power: Kaam karne ki dar (rate of doing work), yaani ek second mein kitna kaam hua ya kitni energy istemal hui.

📐 Power = Kaam / Samay
P = W / t
SI unit: Watt (W). 1 Watt = 1 Joule / 1 second.
(Watt ka naam vaigyanik James Watt ke naam par hai, jinhone steam engine sudhaara.)

Bade kaam ke liye bade units use hote hain: 1 kilowatt (kW) = 1000 watt, aur 1 megawatt (MW) = 10,00,000 watt.

🐎 Horsepower - Purana Unit

James Watt ke zamaane mein log ghodon (horses) se kaam karwate the. Apne steam engine ki taakat samjhaane ke liye Watt ne engine ko ghodon se compare kiya - isse horsepower (hp) unit bana.

📐 1 horsepower (hp) = 746 watt (lagbhag 750 W)

Aaj bhi cars aur pumps ki power aksar horsepower mein batayi jaati hai - jaise "100 hp ki car".

💪 Kya Aap Jaante Hain?

Ek normal insaan continuously lagbhag 75 watt (0.1 hp) ki power de sakta hai. Ek athlete kuch second ke liye 1000+ watt tak pahunch sakta hai! Aur ek bijli ka bulb 5W (LED) se 100W tak ka hota hai.

🧮 Solved Numericals

✔ Example 1: Mazdoor ka kaam

Ek mazdoor 6000 J kaam 30 second mein karta hai. Uski power?
P = W / t = 6000 / 30 = 200 W.

✔ Example 2: Seedhiyan chadhna

50 kg ka ladka 3 m oonchi seedhiyan 5 second mein chadhta hai (g = 10). Power?
Kaam = mgh = 50 × 10 × 3 = 1500 J. Power = 1500 / 5 = 300 W.

✔ Example 3: Kaam nikalna

Ek 60 W ka bulb 10 second tak jalta hai. Kitni energy use hui?
W = P × t = 60 × 10 = 600 J.

⚡ Bijli Ka Bill - Commercial Unit (kWh)

Aapke ghar ka bijli ka bill Joule mein nahi aata - kyunki Joule bahut chhoti unit hai (ek bulb ek din mein laakhon joule kha jaata hai!). Isliye bijli ke bill ke liye ek badi unit use hoti hai: kilowatt-hour (kWh), jise aam bhasha mein "unit" kehte hain.

📌 1 kilowatt-hour (kWh) = 1 "unit" bijli
Ye wo energy hai jo 1 kilowatt (1000 W) ka device 1 ghante (1 hour) tak chalne par kharch karta hai.
Joule mein: 1 kWh = 1000 W × 3600 s = 3.6 × 10⁶ J = 36,00,000 J.

Bill nikalne ka formula bilkul aasaan hai:

📐 Units (kWh) = (Power in kW) × (Hours use kiye)
Bill = Units × Rate per unit (₹)

✔ Example 4: Ek AC ka bill

1500 W (= 1.5 kW) ka AC roz 8 ghante, poora mahina (30 din) chalta hai. Rate ₹6/unit.
Roz ki units = 1.5 × 8 = 12 kWh.
Mahine ki units = 12 × 30 = 360 kWh (units).
Bill = 360 × 6 = ₹2160.

✔ Example 5: LED bulb kitna sasta hai

Ek 100 W ka purana bulb vs 10 W ka LED, dono roz 10 ghante, 30 din (rate ₹6/unit):
Purana: 0.1 kW × 10 × 30 = 30 units → ₹180/mahina.
LED: 0.01 kW × 10 × 30 = 3 units → ₹18/mahina.
LED se ₹162 har mahine ki bachat - aur bijli bhi bachi!

🔌 Power Rating Kahan Dekhein

Har electrical device par uski power rating likhi hoti hai (jaise "60 W", "1.5 ton / 1500 W"). Isse aap andaza laga sakte ho ki wo kitni bijli khaayega:

DeviceLagbhag Power
LED bulb7 - 12 W
Ceiling fan (pankha)70 - 80 W
TV (LED)80 - 150 W
Fridge150 - 300 W
Washing machine500 W
Air conditioner (AC)1000 - 2000 W
Geyser / iron (istri)1500 - 2000 W
🇮🇳 India Connection

Bharat sarkar ka BEE Star Rating (wo 1 se 5 stars wala label jo AC, fridge par hota hai) power-saving batata hai - jitne zyada stars, utni kam bijli. India ka UJALA scheme ke tehat 36 crore se zyada LED bulb baante gaye, jisse desh ne arbon units bijli aur hazaron crore rupaye bachaaye. Power samajhna sirf physics nahi - paise aur planet dono bachata hai!
